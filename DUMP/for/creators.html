DUMP for creators

A week of content, from photos you already took.

You shoot all week and post on Sunday, if you remember. DUMP turns that into a routine: every Sunday it drafts your week from the last seven days, on your iPhone. You check it, make it look like you, and post the carousel (or a swipe-video Reel).

For creators, small brands and anyone who wants to post consistently without the picking.

The posts aren't hard. The choosing is.

Three things that quietly kill posting streaks, and what DUMP does about each one.

“I shoot all week and post nothing.”

Sunday draft. Every Sunday evening, "Your week" is waiting: the best shots of the last seven days, best one first. You review instead of starting from zero.

“Picking takes longer than editing.”

Smart Dump. Drops blurry shots and burst repeats, mixes people, places and details, and keeps time order, on your iPhone. From this weekend, last month, this year or a trip it found.

“My feed looks random.”

A repeatable look. Templates you fill in one tap, 28 grids, frames, film-style date stamps and seamless panoramas keep every week recognisably yours.

The routine

One recurring post. Zero blank pages.

Shoot casually Monday to Saturday. On Sunday, check the draft, caption it and post. That's the whole system.

  • MonShoot a morning detail
  • TueYour desk or set
  • WedBehind the scenes
  • ThuA person or a client moment
  • FriSomething you made
  • SatThe big moment + a short video
  • SunDUMP drafts your week. Check, caption, post.

The free plan keeps 5 projects at a time. Saved albums stay in Photos when you delete an old project in DUMP, so the weekly habit works for free if you clear old projects; Premium removes the limit.

Start from a draft, not a blank canvas

DUMP's Sunday notification opens a ready draft of your week. Missed a week? Make one in a couple of taps with Smart Dump and a moment: this weekend, last month, this year or a detected trip.

Make it look designed in one tap

Pick a template, tap Dump It, and DUMP fills every empty slot from your library. Re-dump until it feels right; every change can be undone. Then add your consistent touch: frames, date stamps, cut-out stickers, text styles.

  • Collage pages (up to 20 per project), 28 grid layouts, seamless panoramas
  • 40+ frames and 500+ stickers; cut-outs lifted on-device
  • Bookmark the templates you reuse every week

One dump, two posts Premium

Post the carousel, then export the same dump as one swiping video for Reels (10 to 30 seconds, 1080 px wide). Stuck on the caption? The Caption Generator offers 60 hand-written lines in six moods. It isn't AI and doesn't read your photos.

  • Swipe video export for Reels
  • Caption starters: Soft, Funny, Travel, Monthly Recap, Influencer, Minimal
  • Copy captions without the #DumpApp tag
Before you install

What DUMP doesn't do.

  • No auto-posting or scheduling. Instagram only opens carousel publishing to approved tools for professional accounts, through its API. DUMP has no account or server; it opens the composer and the last tap is yours.
  • No analytics. Use Instagram's own insights for that.
  • No AI captions. The Caption Generator is hand-written lines (Premium).
  • No video trimming, no TikTok share button, and no 3:4 canvas preset (4:5, 1:1, 1.91:1 and 9:16 are built in; set Ratio → Other to 3 × 4).
  • iPhone and iPad only. No Android or web version.

FAQ

Creator questions

Can DUMP post to Instagram for me?

+

No. Instagram only lets approved scheduling tools publish carousels to professional accounts through its API; DUMP has no account or server, so the last tap is yours. DUMP saves your dump to a new Photos album in posting order and can open Instagram's composer for you; you pick the album, tap the slides in order and post.

How does the Sunday draft pick my photos?

+

On Sunday evening DUMP looks at your last seven days on your iPhone, picks the best shots, puts the strongest one first as the cover and keeps the rest in the order you took them. It needs reminders and notifications turned on. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I turn a dump into a Reel?

+

Yes, with Premium. DUMP exports the whole dump as one swiping video, 10 to 30 seconds long and 1080 px wide. Add music in Instagram when you post it.

Will my posts have a DUMP watermark?

+

No. Exported images and videos have no watermark on the free plan or Premium. On the free plan, captions you copy can include a small #DumpApp tag; Premium lets you copy them without it.

How much does DUMP cost for creators?

+

DUMP is free: Random Dump, Smart Dump and the Sunday draft build up to 10 items, and you can keep 5 saved projects. Optional Premium (weekly or yearly plan, price on the App Store) adds 20-slide auto-built dumps, unlimited projects, every template, frame, sticker, font and filter, the Caption Generator and swipe-video export.
