Help centre

Questions, answered.

Eighteen straight answers about quality, privacy, Premium and posting. Nothing here needs an account to read — or to use.

DUMP is a photo and video dump maker for iPhone. It turns your camera roll into a share-ready Instagram carousel, collage or grid, then saves the finished result into a new Photos album in the exact order you built it. It works fully offline and needs no account.
A photo dump is a casual carousel post made of several real, unpolished photos and videos posted together instead of one perfect shot. It is the format people use for weekend recaps, trips, month-in-review posts and anything that works better as a story than a single image.
Swipe Carousel mode gives each photo or video its own slide and exports them untouched, with zero recompression. Canvas mode is a freeform multi-page editor where you place, layer, mask and decorate media on a page, then render it. Use carousel mode to post fast, canvas mode to build something custom. Yes. Photos and videos live side by side in the same dump. Videos keep their original quality in Swipe Carousel mode, and inside Canvas pages they can be trimmed, muted and played back in place.
DUMP saves the finished dump into a new album in Apple Photos, named after your project and already in the right order. Open Instagram, create a post, choose that album, tap select-multiple, select everything, and post. No reordering is needed.
DUMP is built for iPhone running iOS 17 or later. It can be installed on iPad and will run in iPhone compatibility mode, but the layout is designed for phone screens. There is no Android or web version.

Projects are stored on your device only, so deleting DUMP deletes them permanently and they cannot be recovered. Anything you have already exported stays safe in Apple Photos, because those are ordinary photos in your library.
Nothing is uploaded. DUMP has no server, no account and no login. It processes everything on your iPhone and runs perfectly in airplane mode. Your photos and videos never leave the device. Read the Privacy Policy →
Photos access is needed for two things only: reading the assets you choose to put in a dump, and writing your finished dump into a new album. DUMP does not scan, index, catalogue or transmit your library.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside DUMP. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select DUMP and choose cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next renewal.
All purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple rather than from the developer. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the DUMP purchase and submit a request.
Open Settings inside DUMP and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase and connected to the internet. If it is still locked afterwards, email solostaseraofficial@gmail.com with your purchase date and Apple receipt.
