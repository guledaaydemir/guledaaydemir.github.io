Learn the basics

Watch the whole app in six scrolls.

Every step below is the real flow, animated. Keep scrolling — the phone follows you.

Camera roll
01 — Start

Your camera roll, narrowed down

Point DUMP at Favourites, a single album, or the whole library. It pulls eligible photos and videos, skips duplicates, and hands you a dump you can actually post instead of a folder you have to sort.

02 — Random Dump

One tap. A whole carousel.

Random Dump builds the full sequence instantly. Don't like slot three? Shuffle just that slot, or replace it by hand. Order stays exactly where you put it.

03 — Templates

A ready-made look, filled with your photos

Pick a template and your media pours into it — spacing, ratios and layers already balanced. Save any page you build as your own reusable template.

04 — Grids

Many photos, one slide

Drop several shots into a grid, then dial spacing, corners and ratio until it sits right. Perfect for recaps, before-and-afters, and month-in-review posts.

05 — Canvas

Or build it exactly your way

Freeform pages with frames, stickers, textures, text styles, filters, crop and masks. Multi-page projects and seamless panoramic carousels that swipe across slides without a seam.

06 — Export

Straight into Photos, in order

DUMP creates a new album named after your project and saves everything in sequence at full resolution. Open Instagram, select all, post. Done.
