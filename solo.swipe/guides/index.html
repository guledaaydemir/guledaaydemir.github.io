Cleaning with Solo.Swipe

Solo.Swipe is a photo cleaner and organizer for iPhone and iPad, built around swipe gestures. It shows your photos one at a time as full-screen cards: swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it, up to file it into an album, and down to skip it. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

It splits a large library into finishable sets — a day, an album, a city, screenshots, videos — and its Smart Modes surface likely blurry shots and photos where someone's eyes look closed (you check each one) using Apple's on-device Vision framework, while a Duplicates view handles exact copies (Smart Modes, Duplicates and Large Files are Pro, or free for a day after a short ad). Photos are analysed on your device and are never uploaded, which you can read about in the privacy policy or on the app information page.
