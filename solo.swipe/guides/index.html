Cleaning with Solo.Swipe

Solo.Swipe is an iPhone photo cleaner and organizer built around swipe gestures. It shows your photos one at a time as full-screen cards: swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, right to keep it, up to file it into an album, and down to skip it. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

It splits a large library into finishable sets — a day, an album, a location, screenshots, videos, your largest files — and its Smart Modes surface blurry shots, closed-eye photos and duplicate groups using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Photos are analysed on your iPhone and are never uploaded, which you can read about in the privacy policy or on the app information page.
