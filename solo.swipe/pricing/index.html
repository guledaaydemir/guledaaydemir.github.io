Solo.Swipe Pricing: Free vs Pro

By Gül Eda Aydemir, indie iOS developer · Updated 15 Oct 2026 for version 5.0 · 4 min read

Quick answer

Solo.Swipe is free to download and use, with ads and a limit of 50 deletions a day. Smart Modes, Duplicates and Large Files are Pro features you can unlock for a day by watching a short ad. Solo.Swipe Pro removes the ads and limits, with a monthly or yearly plan; see the current price on the App Store. There is no weekly plan.

What do you get for free?

The whole swipe-cleaning experience, with ads and a daily deletion limit. Nothing in the free version expires, and you never need an account.

  • Swipe review with Undo, the Final Review screen and the iOS safety net (Recently Deleted for 30 days)
  • 15 of the 18 ways to browse: by day, month and year, Recents, Favorites, albums, cities, Random 20, Random Date, screenshots, selfies, RAW, bursts, videos (with 4K, long, slow-mo, screen recording and other filters) and panoramas
  • Swipe-up album filing, album clean-up and the Empty Albums list
  • Stats, the storage breakdown, the daily space goal, streaks, achievements and all four widgets

The limits, stated plainly:

  • 50 deletions a day. Watch an optional ad to add 20 more, as often as you like.
  • Ads in the deck. An ad card appears after your first 5 swipes and then every 5 swipes.
  • Smart Modes, Duplicates and Large Files are Pro. Watch a short ad to unlock each one for the rest of the day.

What does Solo.Swipe Pro add?

No ads, no daily limit, and the three Pro tools whenever you want them.

Where can I see the price of Solo.Swipe Pro?

On the App Store, in your own currency. Pro comes as a monthly or a yearly plan; see the current price on the App Store or on the subscription screen in the app before you confirm. There is no weekly plan and no lifetime purchase.

  • Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, charged to your Apple Account when you confirm.
  • It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Any free trial or introductory price is shown on the subscription screen before you confirm.
  • Discounted offers sometimes appear inside the app, for example during seasonal sales.

How do I cancel, manage or restore Pro?

In your Apple Account settings, not in the app. Apple handles all billing, so Solo.Swipe never sees your payment details.

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Solo.Swipe.
  3. Choose a different plan or tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

New phone, or Pro not showing up? Open the subscription screen in Solo.Swipe and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Is Solo.Swipe Pro worth it?

Only if you'll use what it adds. An honest rule of thumb:

  • Stay free if you clean a little each day: 50 deletions is a solid daily session, and watching the occasional ad unlocks a Pro tool when you need it.
  • Consider Pro if you're clearing a big backlog (hundreds of deletions a day), you lean on Smart Modes, Duplicates or Large Files, or the ad cards break your rhythm.
  • Try before you decide: use the free version for a week and see which limit, if any, you actually hit.

Does Pro change what data is collected?

Pro removes the ads; the rest stays the same. Your photos never leave your device on either plan. Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) work the same on both plans; version 5.0 has no in-app switch for them. Details are in the privacy policy.

Start free, upgrade only if you need to

Clear today's photos in a five-minute swipe session. If you hit the daily limit or want Smart Modes every day, Pro is one tap away, and you can cancel any time.

Pricing questions

Is Solo.Swipe free?

Yes. You can download and use Solo.Swipe for free, with ads and a limit of 50 deletions a day (watch an optional ad for 20 more). Smart Modes, Duplicates and Large Files can be unlocked for a day by watching a short ad.

Does Solo.Swipe have a weekly subscription?

No. Solo.Swipe Pro is sold as a monthly or a yearly plan; there is no weekly plan. See the current price on the App Store.

What happens when I reach the daily deletion limit?

You can watch an optional ad to add 20 more deletions, come back tomorrow when the free allowance starts again, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited deletions.

Can I use Smart Modes and Duplicates without paying?

Yes. In the free version, watch a short ad to unlock Smart Modes, Duplicates or Large Files for the rest of the day. Pro unlocks them permanently while your subscription is active.

How do I cancel Solo.Swipe Pro?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Solo.Swipe, and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
