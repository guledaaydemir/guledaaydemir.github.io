How it works

How does Solo.Swipe clean up iPhone photos?

You pick a small set of photos, swipe through them one at a time, then confirm what goes. Most sessions take a few minutes, and every finished set is real progress you can see.

  1. Pick a set you can finish

    One day, one month, Random 20, your screenshots or your biggest videos: there are 18 ways to browse, so you never face the whole library at once. Photos you have already reviewed don’t come back.

  2. Swipe to decide

    Left to delete, right to keep, up to add to an album, down to skip. Videos play and Live Photos move inside the card. Swiped the wrong way? Tap Undo.

  3. Confirm and see the space come back

    A Final Review lists everything you marked, then iOS asks once more. Deleted items wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Your session summary shows what you freed, and your streak ticks up.

What you get

What Solo.Swipe does for your camera roll

Each tool is built around a job you actually have: free up space, catch the bad shots, organize the keepers, and keep it tidy without thinking about it.

Storage full?

Get gigabytes back, starting with the biggest files

Videos usually take far more space than photos, so start there. Large Files lists your 20 largest videos or photos. Video filters pick out 4K clips, long videos (over 5 minutes) and screen recordings. The storage breakdown shows whether videos, screenshots or selfies take the most room, with a one-tap “Clean 20 now”. Every session ends with how much space you freed.

Pro feature. Free users can unlock Large Files for the rest of the day by watching a short ad.

Smart Modes

A detective for the bad shots

Smart Modes use Apple’s Vision and Core Image frameworks, on your device, to pull out photos worth a second look. You still decide on every photo:

  • Blurry Photos lists up to 20 photos on your iPhone that look soft, blurriest first. It measures sharpness across the whole frame, so check Portrait-mode shots and soft-background close-ups before you swipe them away.
  • Closed Eyes finds photos where someone’s eyes look closed (it ignores small faces in the background). It doesn’t check whether a better retake exists, so before you swipe left, make sure it isn’t the only photo of that moment.
  • Seven more detectives: Documents & Receipts, Text & Notes, Pets, Plants & Nature, Food & Dining, Sunsets & Sunrises, and Vehicles.

Pro feature. Free users can unlock Smart Modes for the rest of the day by watching a short ad. You still decide on every photo.

Duplicates

Exact duplicates, cleared in pairs

Duplicates finds photos saved twice: the same moment, captured in the same second at the same size. Each pair appears side by side. Tap the one to keep, choose Merge to keep the larger file, or Delete Both. Undo is one tap away.

Honest scope: it matches exact copies, not similar-looking shots. For burst frames and retakes taken seconds apart, open By Day or Burst Photos and swipe through them together.

Pro feature. Free users can unlock Duplicates for the rest of the day by watching a short ad.

Albums

Organize while you clean

Swipe up to drop a keeper into an album, or create a new album on the spot. You finish a session with a smaller library and sorted keepers, without a second pass.

  • Rename albums, move photos between them, or review an album with a swipe.
  • Clean up albums three ways: the album and its photos, the album only (photos stay), or the photos only (album stays).
  • Empty albums get their own list, with Select All.

Making a post? A “Create a post with DUMP” card links to DUMP, Gül Eda’s carousel and photo-dump app, where you build a post from that album’s photos.

Habit

A five-minute habit that keeps it tidy

A camera roll refills every week, so a once-a-year purge never lasts. Solo.Swipe turns clean-up into a small daily routine:

  • Daily space goal. Pick a target from 250 MB to 5 GB and watch the ring fill as you free storage.
  • Streak. Review at least 10 photos a day to keep it. Streak Freezes (hold up to 3) cover busy days.
  • Progress you can see. 20 achievements, a 7- or 30-day activity chart and a cleaning calendar.
  • Widgets (iOS 17+). Your streak on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, a Daily Photo with Shuffle, Space Freed, and Quick Clean.
  • Gentle reminders, scheduled on your device, including On This Day memories.

Privacy, in plain words

What happens to my photos and data?

Your photos and videos never leave your device. Solo.Swipe reads them through Apple’s PhotoKit and analyses them on the device. Some app data does leave it, and this is exactly what.

Stays on your device

  • Your photos and videos, and the result of every scan
  • Your stats, streaks, goals, reviewed list and settings
  • Reminders, which iOS schedules on the device
  • There is no account, so no name, email or password

Leaves your device

  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), tied to a random app ID, not your name, email or Apple Account. If you allow tracking, Google may also link them to your advertising identifier. There’s no in-app switch for this in version 5.0.
  • Ads in the free version from Google AdMob, which uses device and ad data. You get Apple’s tracking prompt before ads can use your advertising identifier.
  • Place names: the GPS coordinates already in a photo are sent to Apple’s geocoder to show the city.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple.

The App Store privacy label for Solo.Swipe lists the data these Google SDKs collect. None of it includes your photos. Read the full privacy policy

Frequently asked questions

What is Solo.Swipe?

Solo.Swipe is a photo cleaner and organizer for iPhone and iPad. It shows your photos one at a time so you can swipe left to delete, right to keep, up to add the photo to an album and down to skip it, in short sessions instead of one exhausting clean-up.

Is Solo.Swipe free?

Yes. Solo.Swipe is free to download and use, with ads. The free version allows 50 deletions a day (watch an optional ad for 20 more) and shows an ad card every 5 swipes. Smart Modes, Duplicates and Large Files can be unlocked for the rest of the day by watching a short ad. Solo.Swipe Pro removes the ads and limits, with a monthly or yearly plan; see the current price on the App Store. There is no weekly plan. Free vs Pro in detail.

Is there a Tinder-style app for deleting photos on iPhone?

Yes. Apple's Photos app has no swipe-to-review mode, but swipe cleaners such as Solo.Swipe do: one photo at a time, swipe left to delete and right to keep. Solo.Swipe adds swipe up to file the photo into an album, swipe down to skip it, and an Undo button for the last swipe.

My iPhone storage is full. What should I delete first?

Start with what's big: long or 4K videos and screen recordings, then screenshots and duplicates. Solo.Swipe's Large Files mode lists your 20 largest videos so you can swipe through those first (Pro, or unlock it for the day with an ad). Then empty Recently Deleted, because deleted items keep using space for 30 days.

I deleted photos, so why didn't my storage go down?

Deleted photos move to the Recently Deleted album and keep taking up space for 30 days. To get the space back now, open Photos, go to Recently Deleted and delete them there. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions also sync to your other devices. More on freeing up storage.

Does Solo.Swipe upload my photos?

No. Photos and videos are read through Apple's PhotoKit and analysed on your device with Apple's Vision framework; they are never uploaded. The app does send usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase (version 5.0 has no in-app switch for this), and the free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.

Does Solo.Swipe find duplicate photos?

Yes, exact duplicates: photos captured in the same second at the same size, shown as pairs. For each pair you can keep one, Merge (which keeps the larger file) or Delete Both. It does not group similar-looking shots; for retakes and burst frames, browse By Day or Burst Photos and swipe through them together.

How does Solo.Swipe find blurry photos?

Its Blurry Photos mode lists up to 20 photos on your iPhone that look soft, blurriest first. It measures sharpness across the whole frame, so check Portrait-mode shots and soft-background close-ups before you swipe them away. You still decide on every photo.

Will Solo.Swipe suggest deleting photos where someone blinked?

It can show them to you, and you decide. The Closed Eyes mode finds photos where someone's eyes look closed (it ignores small faces in the background). It doesn't check whether a better retake exists, so before you swipe left, make sure it isn't the only photo of that moment. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

Does Solo.Swipe work on iPad, on a Mac and with VoiceOver?

Yes. It is a universal iPhone and iPad app (iOS or iPadOS 16 or later), and it also runs on Macs with Apple silicon and on Apple Vision Pro as an iPad app. On the swipe deck, Keep, Delete, Album and Skip are available as VoiceOver actions.

How do I make photo cleanup a habit?

Keep it small and daily: five minutes with one finishable batch beats a once-a-year purge. In Solo.Swipe, reviewing at least 10 photos a day keeps your streak alive, a daily space goal ring shows how much you freed, and widgets can put your streak on the Home Screen or Lock Screen. We call this routine a Camera Roll Reset. The Camera Roll Reset routine.

If I delete the app, will my photos be deleted?

No. Solo.Swipe works on your Apple Photos library, and deleting the app leaves your photos and albums untouched. Only Solo.Swipe's own data, such as your stats and streaks stored on the device, is removed with the app.
