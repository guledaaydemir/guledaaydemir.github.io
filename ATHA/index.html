Apple Health

Your breathing can live
with your health data.

Health integration is optional and controlled by you. When enabled, completed breathing sessions can be saved as Mindful Sessions, and reflections can be written as State of Mind entries where supported.

Mindful Sessions

Save the duration of completed ATHA sessions to Apple Health after you finish.

Optional connection

State of Mind

When you choose to share a reflection, ATHA can write that feeling to Apple Health on supported iOS versions.

Private by design

No ATHA cloud.
Your space stays yours.

ATHA is designed without a developer-controlled cloud backend for your breathing history. Core practice data stays on your device. Apple Health data is only written when you explicitly enable Health access.
