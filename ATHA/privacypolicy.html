Private by design

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026 · Last updated: September 9, 2026
ATHA is designed around a simple principle: your breathing practice should stay personal. ATHA does not operate a developer-controlled cloud backend for your breathing history. Core practice data is stored on your device. Optional Apple Health features only write data after you explicitly grant permission.
01 — Who we are

About ATHA

ATHA ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is developed and operated by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent iOS developer. This Privacy Policy explains how ATHA handles information when you use the App.

02 — Data we do not collect

What ATHA does not do

We want to be explicit about the privacy model:

  • We do not operate a developer-controlled cloud database containing your breathing history.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your breathing, reflection, or Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not receive your payment card information when you purchase through the App Store.
  • We do not require Health access to use core breathing features.
03 — Data stored on your device

Core ATHA data

ATHA may store the following information locally on your device using Apple platform storage technologies:

  • Breathing session history — dates, duration, selected practice, completion status, and related session metadata.
  • Custom breathing patterns — names and inhale / hold / exhale / hold-out timings you create.
  • Routines and reminder preferences — routine settings, local reminder times, and notification preferences.
  • Favorites and recent practices.
  • Progress information — streaks, practice days, garden stage, rewards, records, and statistics derived from your activity.
  • Reflections and check-ins you choose to record in ATHA.
  • App preferences such as theme, appearance, sound, haptic, visual, accessibility, onboarding, and feature settings.
No ATHA cloud: ATHA does not provide its own cloud-sync account for this data. Your operating system may still include app data in device backups depending on your Apple / iCloud backup settings. Those backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by an ATHA server.
04 — Apple Health

Optional HealthKit integration

ATHA can integrate with Apple Health only after you enable the relevant controls and grant permission through iOS.

Mindful Sessions Optional

When Save sessions is enabled, ATHA can write completed breathing session duration to Apple Health as a Mindful Session.

State of Mind Optional

When Share reflections is enabled and supported by your iOS version, ATHA can write reflections you choose to share as State of Mind entries.

Health permissions are managed by iOS. You can review or revoke them at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings. Data written to Apple Health is stored and managed by Apple's Health ecosystem according to your device, Health, and iCloud settings.

ATHA does not use Health data for advertising, profiling for ad networks, or sale to data brokers.

05 — Notifications

Local reminders

If you enable reminders, ATHA requests permission to send notifications. Routine, daily, and streak-related reminders are intended to be scheduled locally on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

06 — Purchases

Apple StoreKit / App Store

If ATHA offers paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases, transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Billing, purchase history, refunds, and subscription management are governed by your Apple ID and Apple's terms and privacy practices.

07 — Diagnostics

Crash and App Store analytics

Apple may provide developers with crash reports, performance diagnostics, and aggregated App Store analytics depending on your Apple analytics-sharing choices. These reports are used to improve stability and performance. ATHA does not use this information to build a marketing profile about your breathing practice.

08 — Website

ATHA website

This website is a static product and support site. The App's on-device privacy model does not automatically apply to third-party infrastructure used to host the website. If analytics, forms, cookies, or other website services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated before those services are enabled.

09 — Your choices

Control and deletion

  • Health: Review or revoke ATHA's Apple Health permissions through Apple's Health permission controls.
  • Notifications: Disable notification access in iOS Settings.
  • Local ATHA data: Use any in-app deletion controls that are available. Deleting the App may remove locally stored ATHA data from the device, subject to your device backup settings.
  • Apple Health entries: Data already written to Apple Health is managed separately in the Health app and may remain there after ATHA is deleted.
  • Purchases: Manage subscriptions and purchase history through your Apple ID / App Store settings.
10 — Children's privacy

Age considerations

ATHA is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated account or cloud database. If applicable law in your region imposes additional age or consent requirements, those requirements apply to your use of the App.

11 — Security

Device-level protection

Because core ATHA data is stored on your device, its protection relies in part on iOS security features and your device security settings. We recommend keeping iOS up to date and using a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where available.

12 — Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ATHA evolves or as legal and platform requirements change. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or an App Store update note.
