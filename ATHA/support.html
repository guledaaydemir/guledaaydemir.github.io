Getting started

Which devices and iOS versions does ATHA support?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18.6 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10.6 or later. In ATHA 1.0, widgets and the Live Activity need iOS 26.1 or later. ATHA is a free download from the App Store. It is not verified for Mac.

How do I start my first session?

Open ATHA and tap Breathe now. Or tap the check-in to say how you feel, or choose a moment such as Before a meeting or Before sleep, and ATHA picks the breath. You can also browse every pattern in Explore.

How do I change the language?

ATHA is available in English and Turkish. Open Settings in ATHA and choose Language; you can follow the system language or pick one for the app only.

Apple Health

How do I connect ATHA to Apple Health?

  1. Open ATHA’s Settings.
  2. Go to Apple Health.
  3. Turn on Save sessions to write completed sessions as Mindful Minutes.
  4. Turn on Share reflections if you want reflections written as State of Mind.
  5. Approve the permissions in the iOS sheet.

When permission is granted, the status shows Connected.

Why does Apple Health show “Not connected”?

Check that at least one Health option is on in ATHA and that iOS permission was granted. If you denied permission before, turning the switch on again may not show a new prompt; change it in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.

What does ATHA write to Apple Health? Does it read anything?

When enabled, ATHA writes completed session time as Mindful Minutes and, if you choose, reflections as State of Mind. ATHA does not read any Health data.

Can I use ATHA without Apple Health?

Yes. Health is optional. Breathing, custom patterns, routines, the Garden and insights all work without it.

Sessions, sound and routines

Can I breathe with my eyes closed?

Yes. During a session, tap the eye button until you reach Eyes closed: the screen dims and haptic taps (plus a soft tone, if Full Guided Mode is on) carry the rhythm. Keep haptics or tones switched on, otherwise there is nothing to follow. On silent (Ring/Silent switch), ATHA 1.0 plays no sound, so keep haptics on. In ATHA 1.0 the cues stop if your iPhone locks itself, so for longer sessions set Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock to longer than the session, or use Apple Watch, which keeps going with your wrist down. Minimal view keeps only the visual, the instruction and the time.

Is there a spoken guided voice?

Not in ATHA 1.0. For a sound at every phase, open ATHA’s Settings › Breathing sound, turn on Full Guided Mode (“a sound marks every step of the breath”) and pick Chime, Marimba, Bowl, Drop or Pulse. Haptic strength (Off, Light, Medium or Strong) is also in Settings. A spoken guided voice and ambient sounds are planned for version 2.0.

How do I create a custom breathing pattern?

Open Explore and choose Create your own. Name the pattern, then set Inhale, Hold in, Exhale and Hold out. Holds can be 0, but a pattern needs an inhale and an exhale. Sessions can be 1 to 60 minutes.

How do routines work?

A routine brings you back to a practice at a set time. Create one, choose the practice, time, days and length, and turn on the reminder if you want a notification.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

  • Check that ATHA has notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Check that the reminder or routine is switched on in ATHA.
  • Check the time and your Focus settings.
  • After a time-zone change, open ATHA once so reminders can reschedule.

How do I turn off the streak reminder?

Once notifications are allowed, ATHA sends a gentle streak reminder by default. Turn it off in ATHA’s Settings › Notifications.

Why didn’t a short session count?

Sessions shorter than 60 seconds are not added to your stats, so a mis-tap doesn’t count as practice.

Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

Why is the ATHA Watch app empty?

Open ATHA on your iPhone once while your Watch is nearby. Your favourites (up to five), today’s Daily Breath and I need a moment then sync to the Watch; until that first sync it offers Quick Reset, Coherence and Box Breathing. To see a practice on your wrist, mark it as a favourite on your iPhone. Watch sessions run at the practice’s default length and are sent back to your iPhone history.

Which widgets are there?

Quick Breath, Streak, Daily Progress and Check In (“How are you feeling?”), for the Home Screen and Lock Screen. Long-press the Home or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, and add ATHA. In ATHA 1.0, widgets need iOS 26.1 or later (the app itself runs on iOS 18.6).

What can I say to Siri?

“Breathe now with ATHA”, “Quick reset with ATHA”, “Start my daily breath in ATHA” or “Start a breathing session in ATHA”. You can also add these as Shortcuts.

Can I see the session on my Lock Screen?

Yes, on iOS 26.1 or later in ATHA 1.0. Sessions of 3 minutes or more can show as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Insights and progress

What statistics does ATHA show?

Mindful minutes, sessions, active days, current and longest streak, average session and completion rate, over 7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 1 year or all time, with a calendar, weekly and monthly recaps and your favourite practices.

What does “what works for you” mean?

It comes from your own ratings after sessions, for example how often you felt better after a practice. It is calculated on your phone and is a personal pattern, not a medical measurement.

Why are some insights not showing yet?

Personal insights appear after at least 5 sessions, and time-of-day insights after at least 10, so ATHA doesn’t draw conclusions from one or two sessions.

What is the Garden?

A visual way to see your practice add up. As your total minutes grow, the scene moves from seed to sprout, plant, tree, garden and forest, with an estimate of the air you’ve breathed. It is a metaphor; nothing real is planted.

Privacy and data

Does ATHA collect any data?

No. ATHA has no account and makes no network requests. It contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, so nothing about your use is sent to the developer or anyone else. Apple’s App Store privacy label for ATHA reads “Data Not Collected”. ATHA 1.0 shows an “Anonymous usage data” switch in Settings › Data. It has no effect: the App Store build has no analytics destination, so nothing is sent whether it is on or off. Full details: Privacy Policy.

Where is my history stored?

On your device only. There is no ATHA cloud or iCloud sync; your data is included in your normal device backup, which Apple and your settings control.

How do I delete my ATHA history?

In ATHA, go to Settings › Data › Reset progress. Deleting the app also removes its local data (subject to your backups). Entries already written to Apple Health stay in the Health app until you delete them there.

Does ATHA sell my health or breathing data?

No. ATHA never receives your data, so there is nothing to sell, rent or share.

Pricing

Does ATHA cost anything?

No. ATHA is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads. Every feature is included.

I saw a payment prompt. What should I do?

ATHA 1.0 has no in-app purchases, so you should never see one. If you do, please email a screenshot so it can be fixed.

Will ATHA stay free?

There is no paid tier today. If that ever changes, it will be announced on the website before the update that changes it.
