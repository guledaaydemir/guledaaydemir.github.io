4-7-8 breathing for sleep: how to do it properly

Short answer

Exhale fully. Inhale through your nose for four seconds, hold for seven, then exhale slowly through your mouth for eight. Repeat four times to begin with, building to eight as it gets comfortable. Keep the inhale small — about seventy percent of capacity — because a full inhale before a seven-second hold is what makes people dizzy and conclude the technique doesn't work.

The exact method

  1. Empty your lungsOne full exhale through the mouth before the first cycle. Skipping this is why the first inhale often feels like a gulp.
  2. Inhale for fourThrough the nose, quietly, belly rising. Aim for about seventy percent full, not maximum.
  3. Hold for sevenShoulders down, jaw loose, no bracing. The hold should feel like pausing, not like gripping.
  4. Exhale for eightThrough the mouth, lips slightly pursed. Let the air leave; do not push it out.
  5. Repeat four to eight timesFour cycles takes just over a minute. Stop early if you feel lightheaded.

The four mistakes that stop it working

  • Inhaling too deeply. The most common error by far. A maximal inhale plus a seven-second hold produces air hunger and dizziness. Seventy percent is the target.
  • Rushing the count. Anxiety speeds up counting. If four cycles take you under a minute, your seconds have shrunk. Use a guide rather than your own count.
  • Forcing the exhale. Blowing air out hard recruits the muscles you are trying to relax. Release, don't expel.
  • Doing too many cycles. Eight is plenty. Twenty rounds of an effortful pattern is a workout, not a wind-down.

If 4-7-8 doesn't suit you

The seven-second hold is what makes this pattern strong, and also what makes it unsuitable for some people. If holding your breath triggers tension, panic, or a feeling of being unable to get enough air, do not persist with it — you will train exactly the wrong association.

When to use it

  • In bed, lights off. The primary use. Run it once you are already lying in your sleeping position.
  • After waking at 3am. Especially effective when the problem is a mind that has switched on rather than a body that is alert.
  • Before something stressful. It works outside bedtime too, though four cycles is enough — you want composed, not drowsy.
  • Not while driving or standing. Any breath-hold pattern should be done seated or lying down.

Common questions

How many rounds of 4-7-8 should I do?

Start with four and build toward eight. More is not better — the hold makes this demanding, and pushing past comfort works against the calming effect.

Why does 4-7-8 breathing make me dizzy?

Almost always because the inhale is too large. Inhale to about seventy percent, or shorten the hold to four or five seconds.

Is it seconds or counts?

Seconds, but the ratio matters most. If eight seconds strains, halve everything and keep the proportions. A strained count raises tension and defeats the purpose.
