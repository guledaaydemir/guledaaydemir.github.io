Features

Everything ATHA does, all of it free

Practice

Start breathing in one or two taps

  • ◎

    10 guided breathing patterns

    Box, 4-7-8, Coherence (5-5), Extended Exhale (4-6), Slow Breathing (6-8) and five more, each with exact timings. See them all →

  • ✦

    Pick your moment

    Tap Breathe now, check in with how you feel (Calm, Stressed, Tired, Anxious, Unfocused, Overwhelmed, Happy) or choose a moment like Before a meeting, Before sleep or After a hard day.

  • ○

    “I need a moment”

    A 3-second settle-in, then a short guided breath. Nothing to choose.

  • ◑

    Eyes-closed and Minimal views

    Eyes closed dims the screen while haptic taps, and optional phase tones, carry the rhythm. Minimal view keeps just the visual, the instruction and the time.

  • ⌧

    Create your own pattern

    Set inhale, hold, exhale and hold-out; any phase can be 0. Name it, save it and run it for 1 to 60 minutes.

  • ✧

    10 ambient visuals

    Aura, Orb, Box, Glow, Ripple, Wave, Liquid, Aurora, Flower and Particles, all moving with the same breath.

  • ♫

    Haptics and sound

    Haptics in three strengths (or off), an optional soft tone at each phase (Full Guided Mode: Chime, Marimba, Bowl, Drop or Pulse) and a choice of completion sounds. In 1.0, sounds are muted when your iPhone is on silent. A spoken guided voice and ambient sounds are planned for version 2.0.

  • ◔

    Routines and reminders

    Pick a practice, a time and the days. A quiet local reminder brings you back.

  • ☀

    Daily Breath

    One suggested practice each day, so there is nothing to decide in the morning.

Progress

See it add up, without a scoreboard

  • ❀

    The Garden

    Your minutes grow a scene from seed to sprout, plant, tree, garden and forest, with an estimate of the air you’ve breathed. A metaphor, not a claim.

  • ❖

    Gentle streaks

    Streak freezes cover a missed day. If a streak ends: “A new streak can begin today. One minute is enough.”

  • ◑

    Insights and recaps

    Mindful minutes, sessions, active days, streaks and completion rate over 7 days to all time, plus weekly and monthly recaps.

  • ☆

    What works for you

    After a few sessions, ATHA shows which practices you rated as helping most, and your best time and length. Worked out on your phone from your own ratings.

  • ◉

    Goals and milestones

    Daily and weekly minute goals kept separate from your streak, and quiet milestones from ten minutes to one hundred hours.

  • ↗

    Share card

    Square or Story format with minutes, sessions and streak only. Your moods and notes stay on your phone.

Apple platform

Made for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

  • ⌚

    Apple Watch

    Haptic guidance that keeps running with your wrist down; sessions sync back to your iPhone history. watchOS 10.6 or later.

  • ▦

    Widgets and Live Activity

    Quick Breath, Streak, Daily Progress and Check In widgets for the Home and Lock Screen. Sessions of 3 minutes or more show on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. In ATHA 1.0, widgets and the Live Activity need iOS 26.1 or later.

  • ❖

    Siri and Shortcuts

    “Breathe now with ATHA”, “Quick reset with ATHA”, “Start my daily breath in ATHA”, or a Shortcut with your chosen practice and length.

  • ♡

    Apple Health, optional

    Save sessions as Mindful Minutes and share reflections as State of Mind. Write-only: ATHA reads nothing. Set it up →

  • ◐

    Themes and appearance

    Eight themes (ATHA, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Lavender, Midnight, Aurora, Sand) with System, Light or Dark appearance.

  • ☉

    Accessibility

    VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion support. Haptic taps, plus optional tones, make every session work without looking.

By design

What ATHA deliberately doesn’t do

These are decisions, not missing features.

—

No account, no server, no analyticsThe app makes no network requests and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. Apple’s privacy label for ATHA reads “Data Not Collected”.

—

No adsNot on the home screen, not between sessions, not on the completion screen.

—

No paywall in the middle of a breathNothing is locked, so you won’t open ATHA in a difficult moment and find what you need behind a purchase.

—

No medical claimsATHA does not diagnose, treat or cure anything. It guides breathing and records what you choose to record.

—

No guilt mechanicsReminders are gentle and optional. The streak reminder can be switched off in Settings › Notifications, and streak freezes and “begin again” make a missed day painless.

●

What it does insteadOpens fast, starts in one or two taps, keeps the count and gets out of the way. See the breathing techniques →

Questions

Quick answers

Is ATHA free?

Yes, entirely. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase. Every pattern, visual, theme, sound, routine, insight and integration (Apple Health, widgets, Apple Watch, Siri) is included, with no ads and no account.

Does ATHA need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no ATHA server. Your sessions, reflections, streaks and settings are stored on your device, and the app makes no network requests.

Does ATHA work on Apple Watch?

Yes. ATHA includes an Apple Watch app (watchOS 10.6 or later) with haptic breathing guidance, plus Home and Lock Screen widgets, a Live Activity (both need iOS 26.1 or later in ATHA 1.0) and Siri shortcuts to start a session without opening the app.

Is ATHA available on iPad?

Yes. ATHA is one free download for iPhone and iPad, on iOS 18.6 or later.
