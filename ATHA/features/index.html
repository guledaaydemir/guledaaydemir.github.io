By design

What ATHA refuses to do

These are decisions, not missing features.

—

No account, no ATHA cloudYour breathing history is not uploaded to a server we control. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak.

—

No ads, everNot in the free version, not between sessions, not on the completion screen.

—

No selling your dataBreathing, reflections, mood and Health data are never sold, rented or traded. Analytics are anonymous and can be switched off.

—

No medical claimsATHA does not diagnose, treat or cure anything. It guides breathing and records what you choose to record.

—

No guilt mechanicsNo punishing streak loss, no push notifications designed to make you anxious about missing a day.

●

What it does insteadOpens fast, starts in two taps, keeps the count, and gets out of the way. See the practices →
