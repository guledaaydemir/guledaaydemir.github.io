Progress photo and photo-a-day apps for iPhone: an honest comparison

Eleven ways to watch something change, from a one-second video diary to apps that line your face up on the eyes. Here's which one fits which goal, including the cases where Solo.Shots isn't the best pick.

By Gül Eda Aydemir, indie iOS developer·Updated 15 Oct 2026·9 min read

Disclosure: I make Solo.Shots, so I'm biased. To keep this fair, every competitor fact below comes from that app's US App Store listing, checked on 15 October 2026, and every app gets a "choose it if" that's meant honestly. No ratings or reviews are used. Prices change; check the App Store before you buy.

Quick answer

Pick by goal. For a video diary of your days: 1 Second Everyday. For body-fat estimates and an AI coach: GainFrame. For before/after comparison views and a lifetime option: ProgressCam. For automatic alignment on the newest iOS: Dayloop or Dittoday. For pregnancy-only content: BumpLapse. For one subject on a daily, weekly or monthly rhythm, posters, and a timelapse built from photos you already have (you, a child, a cat or a dog) on iOS 17.6 or later: Solo.Shots.

Which one should I choose?

The one that matches what you want to see. Here's an honest "choose it if" for each.

Solo.Shots

Choose it if you want one subject in the same frame for months (a bump week by week, a baby's first year, a 90-day fitness journey, a beard, a plant, a pet), with reminders and streaks that follow a daily, weekly or monthly rhythm. It also makes posters and a Day-1-vs-today card, and it can build a timelapse of you, someone else or a cat or dog from photos you already have, analyzed on device. It runs on iOS 17.6 or later. Skip it if you don't want ads in a free version or a monthly subscription for the rest: there's no yearly or lifetime plan.

1 Second Everyday

Choose it if you want a video diary: a second (or a few) of every day, mashed into a movie of your life. Skip it if you want the same subject in the same frame; that isn't what it's built for. Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, and it needs iOS 18.

Selfie A Day

Choose it if you want a simple daily selfie with a ghost image, import from the Camera Roll, Dropbox sync and a Face ID lock, on almost any iPhone (iOS 13). Keep in mind that its latest version was released on 12 June 2025, and it shows a tracking label.

Dayloop

Choose it if you want photos aligned for you automatically (face or full body), you speak one of its 17 languages, and you're on iOS 26. One sequence is free. Keep in mind its weekly plan and tracking label.

Day N

Choose it if you like a calendar for each "journey" (fitness, meals, pets, plants, outfits), stamp styles, a ghost mode and a Home Screen widget. It's available in English, Japanese and Korean. Keep in mind it contains ads and shows a tracking label.

ProgressCam

Choose it if before/after views matter most: side by side, slide to reveal or tap to toggle, with alignment at capture, batch-aligned imports and six export formats. It offers yearly and lifetime options and showed no tracking label. For before/after comparisons, it's the better pick than Solo.Shots, which has no comparison slider.

Dittoday

Choose it if you're on iOS 26 and want every project aligned automatically, a yearly rhythm option, 4K and a one-time $14.99 Pro instead of a subscription.

Lapse: Face Timelapse

Choose it if you'd rather hand-pick the photos (from the system picker or Google Photos) or start from one video, and get a face-aligned film made on device. Monthly, yearly, lifetime and pay-per-export options. Solo.Shots instead finds the person or pet across your library for you.

BumpLapse

Choose it if you want pregnancy information next to your bump photos: weeks pregnant, a due-date countdown widget, week-by-week baby-size comparisons and self-care tips. Keep in mind that its listing describes pregnancy features only, so you'd need another app for the baby's first year.

GainFrame

Choose it if you want fitness analysis: body-fat estimates, muscle-group breakdowns, an AI coach and Apple Health, Hevy and Strava connections. Its listing says photos you send to an AI feature are processed for analysis and not stored on its servers. Solo.Shots doesn't analyze bodies at all.

Apple Photos Memories

Choose it if you want a free music movie of a person, pet, place or event, made from photos you already have. It doesn't line faces up, but it costs nothing and it's already on your iPhone.

Where does Solo.Shots fall short?

Honestly, in a few places:

  • Pricing: Premium is monthly only; see the current price on the App Store. There's no yearly or lifetime plan.
  • Ads and tracking label: the free version shows ads, and the App Store shows a "Data Used to Track You" label for it.
  • No before/after slider. ProgressCam does this well.
  • No automatic alignment for photos you take: it helps you get each photo right while you shoot (ghost, guides, level). Automatic eye alignment is only used for timelapses built from your library.
  • 9 languages, where Dayloop lists 17.
  • No pregnancy information or body analysis. BumpLapse and GainFrame do those.

FAQ

Which progress photo app is free?

Most are free to download with limits. Solo.Shots' free version includes a first project (more unlock after a short ad), up to 100 photos per project and 1080p video after an ad, with a watermark and ads. Dayloop's free version includes one sequence. Apple Photos' Memories is free and built in, but doesn't line photos up.

Is there a progress photo app without a subscription?

Yes. On 15 October 2026, Dittoday sold Pro as a one-time US$14.99 purchase, and ProgressCam (US$44.99) and Lapse (US$49.99) offered lifetime options next to their subscriptions. Solo.Shots Premium is a monthly subscription only.

Which apps work on older iPhones?

Dayloop and Dittoday need iOS 26. Solo.Shots needs iOS 17.6; ProgressCam, Day N and GainFrame need iOS 17; Lapse needs iOS 15.5; Selfie A Day runs on iOS 13.

Which apps don't show a tracking label on the App Store?

On 15 October 2026, 1 Second Everyday, ProgressCam, Dittoday, Lapse and BumpLapse showed no "Data Used to Track You" section. Solo.Shots, Selfie A Day, Dayloop, Day N and GainFrame did. Labels are self-reported by developers and can change.
