How it works

Three taps to a calmer pet routine

PETO keeps the daily jobs of pet care in one friendly place, so nothing is forgotten and nothing is done twice.

1 · Add your pets

Pick a species and a pixel avatar for each pet: dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, fish, reptiles and more. PETO suggests routines that fit.

2 · Set the routines

Choose times for feeding, walks, play and cuddles, how often they repeat and when to remind you. Change anything later.

3 · Tap when it's done

One tap on FEED, WALK, PLAY or CUDDLE logs it, cancels the reminder and updates the status. Everyone at home can see it's done.

Quick questions

What is PETO?

PETO is a pet-care routine app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch with a retro pixel-game look. It reminds you about feeding, walks, playtime, cuddles, medication and vet visits, lets you log care with one tap, keeps a health record for every pet, and has special care for cats (litter, water, toilet) and aquariums.

Is PETO free?

Yes. PETO is free to download, and all core reminders and logging are free for as many pets as you have. Cat care, aquarium care, pet voices and memorial mode are free too. Optional PETO Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) adds family sharing, medication reminders, the vet PDF report, photo avatars, seasonal outfits, trends, themes and icons.

Does PETO have ads or need an account?

No. PETO has no ads, no PETO account and no tracking across apps or websites. Your pets' data is saved on your device; family sharing, if you use it, syncs through your own iCloud. Anonymous usage stats help improve the app and can be turned off in Settings.

Which pets does PETO support?

Thirteen species: dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, ferrets, hedgehogs, birds, fish, turtles, lizards, snakes and other pets, each with eight pixel avatar variants.

Can my family share one pet plan?

Yes, with Family sharing (Premium to create, free to join). Everyone sees who fed, walked or played, so pets don't get fed twice. See the family sharing guide.

Which languages does PETO speak?

The app speaks 13 languages, including English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and Russian, and you can switch inside the app. This website is in English, Turkish and Spanish.
