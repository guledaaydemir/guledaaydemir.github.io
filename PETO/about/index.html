Our story

Why we made PETO

Pets don't need a spreadsheet. They need breakfast on time, a walk, a game and a cuddle, from whoever is home. PETO helps with exactly that.

The problem: a hundred tiny jobs

Looking after a pet is not one big task. It's a hundred tiny ones that repeat every day: breakfast at 7:30, the walk before work, the evening meal, a pill with dinner, flea treatment next week, the vet on Thursday. Each one is easy. Remembering all of them, every day, for every pet, is not.

It gets harder when several people share the care. "Did you feed the cat?" "I thought you did." Some evenings the cat eats twice; some mornings the dog waits for a walk nobody planned.

Why another pet app?

The apps we tried felt like work. They wanted an account before showing anything, filled the screen with ads, or turned a happy pet into a spreadsheet of numbers. Reminders were noisy, and missing one felt like failing a test.

We wanted the opposite: an app you actually enjoy opening, that tells you in one look who needs something, lets you tick it off with one tap, and then gets out of the way.

A cosy pixel game, on purpose

PETO looks like a retro game because routines are easier when they feel light. Pixel pets with friendly status labels like "Full", "Walk time soon" or "Getting hungry", chunky buttons, ON/OFF switches and a wooden sign for your weekly insights: it's a small reward each time you care for your pet.

The labels come only from your schedule and what you logged. PETO is a reminder, never a health check, and it says so.

What PETO does for you

  • Remembers for you. Local reminders for feeding, walks, play, cuddles, medication and vet visits, with Fed ✓, Snooze and Skip on the notification.
  • Stops double meals. One tap logs care; with family sharing everyone sees it's done.
  • Keeps the health story. Vaccines with booster reminders, weight over time and vet visits in one record.
  • Makes handovers easy. A one-page care card for the sitter, the neighbour or grandma.
  • Knows cats and fish, too. Litter, water and toilet logs for cats, with a nudge to the vet when something looks off, and tank cleaning every few days for fish and turtles.
  • Speaks with their voice. Record a bark or a meow and that pet's reminders play it.
  • Shows the good days. Weekly insights and a yearly PETO Wrapped of walks, park visits and play.
  • Is gentle at the end. When a pet passes away, memorial mode stops every reminder quietly and keeps their story. It is always free.

Why it's good for pets, too

Many animals do well with predictable days: regular meal times, regular walks and regular play. Vets and animal-welfare groups often recommend steady routines and daily enrichment. PETO doesn't decide what your pet needs; your vet does. It just helps your household keep the plan you chose, day after day.

Privacy is part of the design

PETO has no ads, no PETO account and no server of its own. Your pets, routines, logs and health records stay on your device. If you choose family sharing, it syncs through your own iCloud with the people you invite, and PETO still can't read it. Photo avatars are made on your iPhone, pet voices are recorded and kept on it, and walks reach Apple Health only if you allow it. To learn which features people use, PETO counts anonymous usage statistics that never include names, notes, photos or health records; you can switch them off in Settings.

A Turkish touch

PETO speaks 13 languages, Turkish among them, and this website borrows from Turkish craft: kilim borders, İznik tile patterns, tulips and the blue nazar bead. In Türkiye, whole neighbourhoods look after street cats and dogs together, with bowls of food and water on doorsteps. That "we care for them together" spirit is the heart of PETO's family sharing. And when Bayram comes, your pixel pet can wear a little fez or a tulip crown.
