About PETO

What is PETO?

PETO is a pet-care routine app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch with a retro pixel-game look. It reminds you about feeding, walks, playtime, cuddles, medication and vet visits, logs care with one tap and keeps a health record for every pet.

Who makes PETO?

PETO is made by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent iOS developer. You can write to solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.

Which devices does PETO run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, Mac with macOS 14 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. One Premium purchase unlocks it on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple ID.

Which languages is PETO in?

The app is in 13 languages: English (US and UK), Turkish, Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and Russian. Switch under Settings and it changes instantly. This website is in English, Turkish and Spanish.

Which pets can I add?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, ferrets, hedgehogs, birds, fish, turtles, lizards, snakes and other pets such as rats or hermit crabs. Each species has eight pixel avatars.

Can I add more than one pet?

Yes. Every pet has its own routines, logs, appointments and health record, and Home sorts your pets by who needs something next.

Reminders & logging

How do reminders work?

Each routine has times, a repeat rule and a "Remind me" setting (at the time, 10 or 30 minutes before). iOS delivers the reminders locally, even when PETO is closed. From the notification you can tap Fed ✓, Snooze 15 min or Skip.

I'm not getting reminders. What should I check?

Make sure notifications are allowed for PETO (Settings → Notification settings shows "Allowed"), that the master switch and the care type are on, and that "Reminders for this routine" is on in the routine. Then open PETO once so it rebuilds the schedule.

Reminders came at the wrong time after travelling.

Routines follow your device's local clock. After a time-zone change, open PETO once and the reminders are rebuilt.

Do I have to log everything?

No. Logging is optional and reminders work without it. Logs add history, status labels and weekly insights, and logging something cancels its reminder.

What do labels like "Getting hungry" mean?

They are friendly labels based only on your schedule and your logs. PETO doesn't know how your pet feels; it is a reminder, not a health check.

I logged something by mistake.

Open the pet's History (or tap the row in the Calendar), choose the log and edit or delete it.

Family, sitters & sharing

How does family sharing work?

With PETO Premium you create a household and invite people. Pets, routines, logs and health records sync through each person's own iCloud, so everyone sees who fed, walked or played. Invitees join free.

Does everyone in the family need Premium?

No. Only the person who creates the household needs Premium; people who join by opening the invitation link don't.

What is the pet-sitter care card?

A free one-page PDF or image with your pet's routines, times, notes and a phone number, to give to a sitter, neighbour or family member.

What is PETO Wrapped?

A free set of yearly 9:16 story cards about your year with your pets, such as walks taken, park visits and minutes of play, ready to share.

Cats, fish, voices & memories

Does PETO have special features for cats?

Yes, free: litter cleaning reminders, water drunk in ml and a toilet log (pee, poop, stool and warning signs such as straining or blood). A Litter & water card shows today's numbers and a 7-day average and suggests a vet visit when warning signs are logged. See the cat litter and water guide.

Can PETO remind me to clean the fish tank?

Yes. Aquarium care is free for fish and turtles: set tank cleaning every N days, log the % of water changed and whether you cleaned the filter, and the next reminder counts from the day you actually did it. See the aquarium cleaning guide.

Can reminders use my pet's own voice?

Yes. In Settings → Notifications → Pet voices, record up to 8 seconds of your pet's bark, meow or chirp, and that pet's reminders play it as the sound. The recording stays on your device. See the pet voice guide.

What happens in memorial mode?

When a pet passes away, Edit Pet → Memorial mode stops every reminder, nudge and watch entry for that pet quietly, keeps all logs, photos and the health record, and turns the pet's page into a simple memorial you can share. It is free and you can leave it at any time. See the memorial mode guide.

Can I dress up my pet in PETO?

Yes. Seasonal pixel outfits for Halloween, New Year and Bayram fit every pet avatar. Anyone can try them on; keeping one on a pet is part of Premium. See the seasonal outfits guide.

Apple Watch, Siri & more

What does the Apple Watch app do?

It lists your pets by urgency, logs Feed, Walk, Play, Cuddle or Meds with one tap, shows complications for the most urgent pet and tracks walks as walking workouts with time, distance and steps.

Does PETO use Apple Health?

Only on Apple Watch walks, and only if you allow it: PETO reads steps and distance during a walk and saves the walk as a workout. Without Health access the walk is still timed and logged.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say "I fed Buddy in PETO", "How is Buddy?" or "Open Buddy". On iOS 18 or later you can also add a "Log care" control to Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action Button.

What is the Live Activity?

A playtime countdown and play timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Done ✓ button.

Why does my pet wag or purr when I hold it?

Your pixel pet is alive in PETO: press and hold it to pet it and you feel a wag, purr, flutter or nuzzle (on Apple Watch too). It sleeps in a nightcap at night and gets muddy paws after a walk. Reduce Motion keeps it still.

Premium & purchases

What does Premium include?

Family sharing, the medication routine, the A4 vet PDF report, photo → pixel avatars, seasonal outfits, trends and week-over-week comparisons, the Honey, Berry and Blossom themes, extra avatar variants, seven app icons and history export.

Monthly, yearly or lifetime?

Monthly and yearly renew automatically until you cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that never renews. Every plan works on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Prices are shown in the App Store.

I bought Premium but it still shows locked.

Go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases while online, signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → PETO, and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Privacy & data

Where is my data stored?

Your pets' data is stored on your device. If you turn on family sharing, it also syncs through your own iCloud with the people you invite. There is no PETO account and no PETO server.

Does PETO have ads or tracking?

No ads and no tracking across apps or websites, and your data is never sold. PETO counts anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), such as which screens and features are used, that never include pet names, notes, photos or health records. Turn them off in Settings → Data → Share anonymous usage stats.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset all data, or delete the app. Both remove everything PETO stored on the device.

Does PETO give medical advice?

No. PETO is a routine reminder. For anything about your pet's health, please talk to a veterinarian.
