Privacy policy

Short version: PETO keeps your pets' data on your device and has no ads, no account and no tracking. Anonymous usage statistics never include your pets' names, notes, photos or health records, and you can turn them off.

Effective November 17, 2026

  • No ads
  • No account
  • No tracking
  • Pet data on device
  • Opt-out stats

1. Who we are

PETO ("the app") is made by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy covers the PETO app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch and this website. Questions: solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores

Pet profiles, care routines, activity logs (including cat toilet and water logs and aquarium cleans), health records (such as vaccines, weight and vet visits), veterinary appointments, memorial pages, recorded pet voices and your app preferences. They are saved on your device. PETO has no server of its own and I cannot see this data.

3. Family sharing (optional)

If you create or join a household, your pets, routines, logs and health records sync through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) with the people you invite. Apple stores this data under its privacy policy; PETO cannot read it. You can stop sharing or leave the household at any time.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: to deliver the reminders you set. They are scheduled locally by iOS.
  • Photos (add only): to save pixel avatars, care cards and Wrapped cards to your library. Photo avatars are made on your device; the original photo is never uploaded.
  • Microphone: only while you record your pet's voice for reminders. The recording is saved on your device and never uploaded.
  • Health (Apple Watch): only if you allow it, to read steps and distance during a walk and save the walk as a workout in Apple Health.
  • Screen Time: only if you turn on the Screen Time nudge, to notice time spent in apps you choose. This stays on your device.
  • iCloud: only for family sharing.

You can change every permission in iOS Settings.

5. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features help pet owners, PETO uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used: which screens open, which features and care types are used, how many pets and routines you have (as numbers and species only), app settings such as language and theme, and whether Premium is active, plus the device type, system version, country and app version that Firebase collects automatically. These statistics never include pet names, notes, photos, phone numbers, vet or clinic names, recorded voices or your health records. PETO collects no advertising ID, uses nothing for ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. Google processes the statistics for me under its privacy policy; they are kept for up to 14 months. Turn them off at any time in Settings → Data → Share anonymous usage stats; this also clears the analytics data stored on your device.

6. What PETO does not do

No PETO account. No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold.

7. Purchases

PETO Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; PETO only receives a confirmation that Premium is active, never your payment details. Apple may share anonymised, aggregated statistics with developers (for example, how many people came to the App Store page from this website, through the campaign tag in our links).

8. Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all data in PETO, or delete the app. Both remove everything PETO stored on the device. To remove shared data, leave or stop sharing the household first.

9. Children

PETO is a general-audience app for pet owners. It does not ask for names, contact details or accounts from anyone, including children, and its anonymous usage statistics can be turned off in Settings.

10. Not medical advice

Status labels such as "Getting hungry" come from your schedule. They are not health assessments. For your pet's health, consult a veterinarian.

11. This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. The site remembers a few small choices in your own browser storage (theme colour, reading font, ticked checklist items and a closed language hint); they never leave your browser.

12. Your rights

Your pets' data never reaches me, and the anonymous usage statistics are not linked to your name or contact details, so there is nothing I can look up about you to access, correct or delete; you can stop the statistics at any time in Settings. If you email me, I keep the conversation only to help you and delete it on request. You have the rights given by the laws that apply to you, such as the GDPR or the KVKK.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
