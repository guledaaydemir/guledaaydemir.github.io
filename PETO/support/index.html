How can we help?

PETO is made by one independent developer, and every email is read. Most answers are below; if not, write and you'll get a reply.

Write to us

Questions, bugs, ideas or a photo of your pet: email is the fastest way, and every message gets a reply.

Helpful to include

  • Your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch model and system version
  • The PETO version (Settings → About Us)
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened
  • A screenshot if something looks wrong
  • For purchases: which plan, and roughly when you bought it (never send payment details)

Quick fixes

Reminders don't arrive

Check Settings → Notification settings in PETO shows "Allowed" (if it says Denied, tap Open Settings and allow notifications). Make sure the master switch, the care type and "Reminders for this routine" are on, then open PETO once to rebuild the schedule. Focus modes can hide notifications too.

Reminders arrive at the wrong time

Routines follow the device's local clock. After travelling or a time change, open PETO once so reminders are rebuilt.

Premium shows as locked

Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases while online, with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

A family invitation doesn't open

Open the link on the iPhone where PETO is installed, signed in to iCloud. In iOS Settings check that iCloud is on for PETO. Joining is free and needs no Premium.

Apple Watch isn't up to date

Open PETO on the iPhone so it sends the latest snapshot, and keep the watch near the paired iPhone. Logs from the watch arrive when the two connect.

My pet's voice doesn't play

Use Test in Settings → Notifications → Pet voices and lock the phone to hear it like a real reminder. If nothing plays, check that notifications are allowed for PETO, that the ring/silent switch and Focus aren't muting it, and that "Reminders in my pet's voice" is on. To record, PETO needs microphone access in iOS Settings.

A pet passed away and reminders still come

Open the pet, tap Edit → Memorial mode → Create memorial. Every reminder and nudge for that pet stops quietly and all memories are kept. We're very sorry for your loss.

I want to start over

Settings → Reset all data removes everything PETO stored on this device. It can't be undone.
