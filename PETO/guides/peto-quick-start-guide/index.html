Getting started

PETO quick-start guide: add your pets, set reminders and log care in five minutes

By Gül Eda Aydemir · Updated November 18, 2026 · 5 min read

Reading font
Quick answer

To start with PETO, add your pets, keep or adjust the routines it suggests for each species, and set the times, repeat days and “Remind me” offset for each routine. PETO then schedules local reminders on your iPhone, and one tap on FEED, WALK, PLAY or CUDDLE (or on the notification itself) logs the care. Core reminders and logging are free; optional PETO Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) adds extras such as the medication routine, trends and a vet PDF report.

Key points

  • Add each pet; during onboarding PETO suggests routines for its species, which you can keep or change.
  • A routine has one or more times a day, a repeat rule, a “Remind me” offset and its own reminder switch.
  • Tap FEED, WALK, PLAY or CUDDLE, or act on the notification; logging cancels that reminder.
  • Status labels come only from your schedule and your logs, never from medical data.
  • Reminders, logging, vet appointments, weekly stats and the health record are free.

How do I set up PETO in five minutes?

PETO is a pet-care routine app built for iPhone, iPad (iOS 17 or later) and Mac, with an Apple Watch app on the side. Setting it up takes a few short steps, and you don't need an account: there is no PETO account at all, and everything is saved on your device.

The app's own text is in 13 languages, including English, Spanish and Turkish. You can switch the language inside the app, and the change applies instantly.

How do I add my pets to PETO?

Start with one pet; you can add the others later from Settings → “Add pet”. PETO knows 13 species: dog, cat, rabbit, guinea pig, hamster, ferret, hedgehog, bird, fish, turtle, lizard, snake and “other”, for animals such as rats, hermit crabs, snails or sugar gliders. Each pet keeps its own routines, logs, appointments and health record, so a home with a dog, a cat and a rabbit stays tidy.

Every species has 8 pixel avatar variants. The first 4 are free and the others come with Premium, as does saving a pixel avatar made from a photo of your own pet. That photo is turned into pixel art on your device and is never uploaded.

During onboarding, PETO suggests routines that fit each species: feeding, a walk, playtime and attention for a dog; feeding, playtime and attention for a cat; feeding and aquarium care for a fish. They are suggestions, nothing more. Keep what fits your home and change the rest.

How do I set feeding and walk reminders?

Open a pet and look at its Care Plan. Every routine there, such as Feeding, Walk, Playtime or Attention, has an ON/OFF switch for its reminders. Tap a routine to edit it:

  • Times: one or more a day, for example feeding at 7:30 AM and 6:30 PM. Use “Add time” for another one.
  • Repeat: “Every day”, “Weekdays”, “Custom” days, or every 2 to 60 days, counted from when the job was last done.
  • Remind me: “At time”, “10 min before” or “30 min before”.
  • Default portion: optional, for example “1 cup”.

Reminders are local notifications that iOS schedules from your routines; no PETO server is involved. Feeding and walk reminders are time-sensitive. To silence a whole care type, use the switches in Settings → “Care reminders”. If you want to plan the whole day around your pet, read how to build a daily pet-care routine.

How do I log feeding, walks and play with one tap?

Each pet's page has big FEED, WALK, PLAY and CUDDLE buttons, and Home has a row of Quick Actions for the same jobs. One tap logs it. You can also act straight from a reminder: “Fed ✓” (or “Walked ✓” and so on), “Snooze 15 min” or “Skip”. Tapping the notification itself opens the quick log for that pet.

Logging something cancels its reminder, so nobody gets nagged about a meal that has already been served. If you want more detail, add the time, the amount and unit (cup or g), the food, the appetite (“Ate all”, “Ate some”, “Ate little” or “Skipped”) or a note. Walks can record their duration and a park visit, and play records minutes.

Logging is optional. Reminders work even if you never log anything, but your logs are what the status labels, the history and the stats are built from.

PETO tip

Set “Remind me” to 10 min before for meals you need to prepare, and leave quick jobs like cuddles At time. When the reminder pops up, tap “Fed ✓” right on the notification and you're done.

What do PETO's status labels mean?

Each pet card shows a friendly status such as “All good”, “Meal coming up”, “Getting hungry”, “Walk time!”, “Needs a walk”, “Happy & played” or “Wants cuddles”. These labels come only from your schedule and your logs. They remind you of the routine; they are not a judgment of your pet's health and never medical. If something about your pet's health worries you, ask your vet.

Home sorts your pets by who needs something next and greets you with a line like “1 pet needs attention”, so you can see at a glance where to start.

Where do I see the calendar and weekly stats?

The Calendar tab shows a month grid with coloured dots for each care type and a red cross on days with a vet appointment. Filter it by pet and check today's list, where finished items get a tick. Vet appointments are free and come with their own reminders.

The Stats tab holds your weekly insights: meals and walks completed, park visits, play sessions and minutes, and a bar showing what percentage of scheduled meals you logged. You can filter by pet. Only logged activity counts, so a walk you forgot to log is unknown, not missed.

Vaccines, booster reminders and the weight log live in each pet's health record; the health record guide walks you through them.

What is free in PETO and what needs Premium?

PETO is free to download, shows no ads, and its core, reminders and logging, is free. PETO Premium is optional and comes as a monthly or yearly subscription (both renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple ID subscriptions) or as a lifetime one-time purchase that never renews.

What should I do next?

Once your first reminders arrive, you're set up. If other people help with the pets, read how to share pet care with your family, so everyone sees who already fed whom and no one feeds twice. Walking a dog every day? The Apple Watch walks guide shows how to log and track walks from your wrist.

One last note for travellers: routines follow your device's local clock, so open PETO once when you arrive somewhere new and your reminders are rebuilt.

Questions people ask

Is PETO free?

Yes. PETO is free to download and has no ads. Reminders and logging are free, and so are vet appointments, weekly stats and the health record. Optional PETO Premium is sold as monthly, yearly or lifetime and adds extras such as the medication routine, trends and the vet PDF report.

Do I need to log care for reminders to work?

No. Reminders work whether or not you log. Logging is optional, but it cancels that reminder, updates the pet's status label and counts in your weekly stats.

Do I need an account to use PETO?

No. PETO has no account and no server of its own. Your pets, routines and logs are saved on your device. Family sharing, if you turn it on, syncs through your own iCloud.

Which pets can I add to PETO?

PETO supports 13 species: dog, cat, rabbit, guinea pig, hamster, ferret, hedgehog, bird, fish, turtle, lizard, snake and “other”, for animals such as rats, hermit crabs or snails. You can add several pets, each with its own routines and reminders.
