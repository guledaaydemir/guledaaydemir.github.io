How to make your pet's bark or meow your iPhone reminder sound

By Gül Eda Aydemir · Updated November 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

PETO trims the silence and evens out the loudness, and from then on that pet's reminders, follow-ups and nudges play its own voice instead of the default sound. Tap “Test” to get a real reminder in about 3 seconds; the feature is free and the recording stays on your device.

Can your pet's bark or meow be your iPhone reminder sound?

Yes. PETO, a pet-care routine app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that is free to download, lets you record your pet's bark, meow or chirp and use it as the notification sound for that pet's reminders. Open Settings → Notifications → "Pet voices", make sure "Reminders in my pet's voice" is on and tap "Record" next to your pet. From then on, that pet's reminders, follow-ups and nudges play its own voice instead of the default sound.

It's free, it works per pet, and the recording stays on your device. Here's how to set it up, record a clean clip and fix it when you can't hear it.

Why a familiar sound is easier to notice

Phones chime, ping and buzz all day, and a standard alert sounds like every other app. That makes it easy to swipe away without reading. A sound you know well is different: you recognise it before you look at the screen, and you know straight away which pet the reminder is about.

That helps with the jobs that slip on a busy day, like the evening meal, the afternoon walk or ten minutes of play. Hearing your dog's bark at dinner time feels more like a friendly nudge than a beep. It won't turn anyone into a perfect pet parent, but it makes the reminder harder to miss and a little more fun.

How to set up pet voice reminders in PETO, step by step

  1. Open PETO and go to Settings → Notifications → "Pet voices".
  2. Check that "Reminders in my pet's voice" is switched on.
  3. Find your pet in the list and tap "Record". PETO needs microphone access to record, so allow it when your iPhone asks.
  4. When your pet has made its sound, tap "Stop & save". You have up to 8 seconds. PETO trims the silence and evens out the loudness, so there's nothing to edit.
  5. Tap "Play" to listen back. Not happy with it? Tap "Re-record".
  6. Tap "Test". PETO sends a real reminder in about 3 seconds. As the app suggests, lock your phone to hear it like the real thing.

Changed your mind? Tap the bin icon next to the recording and confirm, and that pet's reminders go back to the default sound. You can record a new clip whenever you like.

Give every pet its own reminder voice

Pet voices work per pet, which is where they shine in a busy household. Record the dog's bark, the cat's meow and the budgie's chirp separately. Each pet's reminders, follow-ups and smart nudges then play that pet's own clip, while a pet without a recording simply keeps the default sound. A quiet rabbit like Poppy may be happy with the default anyway.

The result: when your phone barks, it's about the dog; when it meows, it's the cat's turn, and you can tell from the next room. PETO already groups reminders per pet and shows the pet's pixel sprite, so the sound and the picture match. You can still act right from the notification with "Fed ✓", "Snooze 15 min" or "Skip".

Tips for recording a clear bark, meow or chirp

  • Pick a quiet room. Turn off the TV, the washing machine and the cooker hood. Background noise ends up in the clip too.
  • Hold the phone fairly close, without pushing it into your pet's face.
  • Catch a natural moment. Many dogs bark when the doorbell rings or the lead comes out, many cats meow around mealtimes, and many birds chirp in the morning. Start recording just before; the silence is trimmed anyway.
  • Try a treat or a favourite toy if your pet "talks" when it wants something. If your dog knows a "speak" cue, use it.
  • Keep it short and happy. One clear bark or meow is plenty; you don't need all 8 seconds. A cheerful sound makes a nicer reminder than an anxious one.
  • Never frighten or tease your pet to get a sound. If it isn't in the mood, try another day.
PETO tip

Your pet will hear the reminders too, and some pets may prick up their ears or answer back. If yours seems unsettled by its own voice coming from the phone, record a softer sound or delete the clip to go back to the default sound.

Why can't I hear my pet's voice? What to check

If the test or a real reminder stays silent, one of these is usually the reason. PETO points out several of them on the "Pet voices" screen.

  • Notifications are off for PETO. PETO tells you: "Notifications are off for PETO, so the test can't play." In the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications → PETO and turn on Allow Notifications, plus Sounds if you see it. Also check the notifications master switch in PETO's own Settings.
  • Silent mode is on. As the screen notes, your iPhone's ring/silent switch still applies. In Silent mode, iPhone doesn't play alerts or sound effects, though it may still vibrate. Depending on your model, turn it off with the Ring/Silent switch, the Action button, Control Center or Settings → Sounds & Haptics.
  • A Focus is on. Do Not Disturb, Sleep or another Focus can silence notifications. In Settings → Focus, choose the Focus, tap Apps and add PETO to the apps allowed to notify you. PETO's feeding, walk, medication and vet reminders are time-sensitive, and Apple explains that turning on Time Sensitive Notifications for a Focus lets apps send those immediately.
  • Microphone access is off. If you can't record, PETO shows "Microphone access is off" with a button that opens Settings. You can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn PETO on.
  • The volume is low or the clip is faint. Turn up the ringer and alerts volume, and re-record closer to your pet if the clip sounds quiet.

Is my pet's recording private?

Yes. PETO uses the microphone only while you record a pet voice, and the recording stays on your device. PETO has no account and no server of its own; your pets' profiles, routines and logs are saved on the device too.

PETO does collect anonymous usage statistics, such as which screens open and which features are used. They never include pet names, notes or photos, and your recording stays on the phone. You can turn the statistics off in Settings → Data → "Share anonymous usage stats". To remove a voice, delete the recording; Settings → Reset all data, or deleting the app, removes everything PETO stored on the device.

Questions people ask

Can I use my dog's bark as a notification sound on iPhone?

Yes, for PETO's reminders. In PETO, go to Settings → Notifications → “Pet voices” and record up to 8 seconds of your dog's bark. That dog's reminders, follow-ups and nudges then play it instead of the default sound. Sounds from other apps don't change.

Is the pet voice feature in PETO free?

Yes. Pet voices are free, like PETO's core reminders and logging. Optional PETO Premium, sold as monthly, yearly or lifetime, adds extras such as medication routines and seasonal outfits.

Why can't I hear my pet's voice when a PETO reminder arrives?

Check that notifications are allowed for PETO, that Silent mode is off (in Silent mode iPhone doesn't play alert sounds, though it may vibrate) and that no Focus is silencing PETO. Then tap “Test” in Pet voices and lock your phone to hear it.

Can each of my pets have its own reminder sound?

Yes. Recordings are per pet, so the dog's reminders can bark and the cat's can meow. A pet without a recording keeps the default sound.

Does PETO upload my pet's recording?

No. PETO uses the microphone only while you record, and the recording stays on your device. PETO has no account or server of its own; its anonymous usage statistics don't include the recording and can be turned off in Settings → Data.
