How to track dog walks with Apple Watch (and make the daily walk a habit)

By Gül Eda Aydemir · Updated November 18, 2026 · 5 min read

Reading font
Quick answer

With PETO on Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later) you can log a dog walk with one tap, or track it as a HealthKit walking workout that records time, distance and steps. The walk is saved to Apple Health only if you allow it; without Health access, PETO still times the walk and logs it for your dog. Walk reminders reach your wrist with their buttons, and a watch-face complication shows the pet that needs you most.

Key points

  • One tap on Walk logs a walk instantly, with a haptic tap to confirm.
  • Walk tracking runs as a HealthKit walking workout: time, distance and steps.
  • Apple Health access is optional; without it the walk is still timed and logged.
  • Reminders arrive on your wrist with their buttons, and a complication shows the most urgent pet.
  • Logged walks feed your weekly stats and your yearly PETO Wrapped.

Can you track dog walks with Apple Watch?

Yes. With PETO's Apple Watch app (watchOS 10 or later) you can record a dog walk in two ways. A one-tap log simply notes that the walk happened. Walk tracking goes further: PETO runs the walk as a HealthKit walking workout and records the time, distance and steps. Use the quick log when you only need the record, and tracking when you also want to know how long and how far you went.

Either way, the walk lands in your dog's log in PETO, where it updates the status label, cancels the walk reminder and counts toward your weekly stats. Haven't set up a walk routine yet? Start with the PETO quick-start guide, then come back here.

How do I log a walk with one tap on my wrist?

Open PETO on your watch. Your pets are listed by urgency, so whoever needs something soonest is at the top. Pick your dog and tap Walk: PETO asks whether to “Quick log” the walk or “Track walk”. Choose Quick log and a haptic tap confirms that the walk is logged. The same one-tap buttons exist for Feed, Play, Cuddle and Meds (the medication routine is part of PETO Premium).

Reminders reach your wrist too, complete with their buttons. When the walk reminder arrives, tap “Walked ✓” on the notification once you're back, “Snooze 15 min” if you're just about to head out, or “Skip” to let this one go. Walk reminders are marked time-sensitive.

On iPhone, Siri and Shortcuts can log a walk as well, and on iOS 18 or later a “Log care” control can sit in Control Center, on the Lock Screen or on the Action Button.

How does walk tracking with Apple Health work?

When you track a walk, PETO starts a walking workout through HealthKit, Apple's framework for health and fitness data. During the walk it reads steps and distance. When you finish, you get a “Buddy keeps you fit!” summary, and the walk is logged for your dog with its duration.

PETO asks for permission to use Health on your Apple Watch for two things: reading steps and distance during a walk, and saving the walk. You decide:

  • Allow, and each tracked walk is saved to Apple Health as a walking workout, next to the rest of your activity.
  • Don't allow, and PETO still times the walk and logs it for your dog. Nothing is written to Apple Health.

You can change your mind later. Apple's guide to managing Health data shows where: open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your profile, tap Apps under Privacy, choose the app and turn its health categories on or off.

Keep in mind that the watch is on your wrist, so the steps and distance are yours. The walk is logged for your dog, but the workout numbers belong to the person holding the lead.

What does PETO do with my walk data?

PETO has no account, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no server of its own. Your walk logs are saved on your device, and walks reach Apple Health only if you allow it. PETO’s anonymous usage statistics never include pet names, notes, photos or health records, and you can turn them off in Settings → Data → “Share anonymous usage stats”. If you use Family sharing, the household syncs through your own iCloud, and PETO can't read that data either. Settings → “Reset all data”, or deleting the app, removes everything PETO stored on the device.

How do I add a PETO complication to my watch face?

PETO's watch-face complications show your most urgent pet, so a glance at your wrist tells you whether the walk is next. Add one with Apple's steps for adding complications:

  1. With the watch face showing, touch and hold the display, then tap Edit.
  2. Swipe left all the way to the end. If the face offers complications, they're on the last screen.
  3. Tap a complication slot and turn the Digital Crown to choose PETO.
  4. Press the Digital Crown to save, then tap the face to switch to it.

Apple notes that you can open most apps by tapping their complication on the watch face, so the complication can double as a shortcut into PETO.

Where do my walks show up afterwards?

Every walk appears in your dog's Today's Log in PETO, next to meals, play and cuddles. A walk log can include its duration and a park visit, and History can be filtered by Walk or Park to show them all. Logs can be edited or deleted later, so a double tap by mistake is easy to fix.

The Stats tab counts walks completed and park visits in its weekly insights; filter by pet to see one dog's week. Only logged walks count, so a walk you forgot to log is simply unknown, not missed.

At the end of the year, PETO Wrapped (free) turns it all into 9:16 story cards you can share, with lines like “In 2026 you took 7 walks with Buddy · 4 park visits · 3 h 37 min walking”.

How do I make daily dog walks a habit?

How much exercise a dog needs depends on its breed, age, health and fitness. The UK veterinary charity PDSA says walking should be part of every dog's daily routine, and that most dogs need at least one or two walks a day unless their vet advises otherwise. Growing puppies should be introduced to exercise slowly, and older dogs may need a little less. If you're unsure what's right for your dog, ask your vet. Then these habits help the walk actually happen:

  • Give the walk a fixed time. Add a Walk routine with real times, such as 9:00 AM, and let PETO remind you. A walk with a time on it is harder to postpone than “sometime today”. One routine can hold several times, for example a morning and an evening walk.
  • Get a heads-up. Set “Remind me” to 10 or 30 minutes before, so there's time to find the lead and your shoes.
  • Mix brisk and sniffy walks. PDSA notes that a brisk walk burns off energy, while a slow, meandering walk with time to sniff and explore is great for a dog's mental health.
  • Share the job. With Family sharing, everyone sees who already walked the dog and when.
  • Follow the label, not a streak. PETO has no streak pressure. “Walk time soon” and “Needs a walk” are gentle signals from your schedule and logs, nothing more.

For the rest of the day's care, see how to build a daily pet-care routine.

PETO tip

Anchor the walk to something you already do, like getting home from work, and set the routine a few minutes after it. Then let the watch handle the rest: tap “Walked ✓” on your wrist, or track the walk and enjoy the “Buddy keeps you fit!” summary.

Questions people ask

Can Apple Watch track my dog's walk?

Apple Watch measures you, the person wearing it. With PETO, a dog walk is tracked as a HealthKit walking workout on your wrist (time, distance and steps) and logged for your dog in PETO. You can also tap Walk and choose “Quick log” to log a walk without tracking it.

Do I have to give PETO access to Apple Health?

No. Health access is optional. If you allow it, PETO reads steps and distance during a walk and saves the walk to Apple Health. If you don't, PETO still times the walk and logs it for your dog.

Which Apple Watch do I need for PETO?

Any Apple Watch that runs watchOS 10 or later. The PETO app on iPhone needs iOS 17 or later.

How many walks a day does a dog need?

It depends on breed, age, health and fitness. PDSA says most dogs need at least one or two walks a day unless a vet advises otherwise. Ask your vet what suits your dog, then set PETO's Walk routine to match.
