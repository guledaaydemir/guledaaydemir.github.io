Halloween, New Year and Bayram outfits for your pixel pet (and how it comes alive)

By Gül Eda Aydemir · Updated November 18, 2026 · 5 min read

Reading font
Quick answer

PETO, a pet-care routine app for iPhone and iPad, has pixel outfits for your pet's avatar: a witch hat or pumpkin cap for Halloween, a Santa hat, reindeer antlers or party hat for New Year, and a fez, crescent tiara or tulip crown for Bayram (the Turkish name for Eid). Anyone can try them on for free; keeping one on your pet is Premium. The pixel pet is also “alive” for free: press and hold to pet it, and it sleeps in a nightcap at night, yawns awake in the morning and walks around on muddy paws after a walk.

How do you dress up your pet in PETO?

Open your pet, go to Edit Pet and tap "Outfits". The picker shows your pet's pixel avatar with seasonal headwear for Halloween, New Year and Bayram. Tap any outfit to try it on in the preview. Trying on is free for everyone; keeping an outfit on your pet is part of PETO Premium.

  1. Open your pet and go to Edit Pet.
  2. Tap "Outfits".
  3. Tap an outfit to see it on your pet, or "No outfit" for the plain look.
  4. With Premium, tap "Done" to keep it. Without Premium, the button reads "Unlock with Premium".

The outfits are pixel headwear drawn on top of the avatar, so they fit every one of PETO's 104 pixel pets, whether yours is a golden retriever, a tuxedo cat, a white rabbit or a leopard gecko. Your routines don't change at all; your pet just looks festive while you do them.

What is Bayram, and how does PETO know the dates?

Bayram is the Turkish word for a holiday or festival. In Türkiye the two big religious ones are Ramazan Bayramı, the Turkish name for Eid al-Fitr at the end of Ramadan, and Kurban Bayramı, the Turkish name for Eid al-Adha. They're family days: people visit relatives, greet their elders and share sweets, which is why Ramazan Bayramı is also called Şeker Bayramı, the "sugar feast".

Both follow the lunar Islamic calendar, so in the Gregorian calendar they move about 11 days earlier every year. PETO works the dates out with the Umm al-Qura calendar and features the Bayram outfits from a week before each Bayram, so you don't have to look anything up. The fez, crescent tiara and tulip crown nod to Turkish style; the tulip, for instance, is a classic motif in İznik tiles.

Try outfits on for free, keep one with Premium

Trying on is open to everyone, with or without Premium, so you can see your dog in reindeer antlers before deciding anything. In the picker, each season carries a Premium badge and the outfits show small locks; they're about keeping an outfit, not trying it on.

PETO Premium is optional and sold as monthly, yearly (both renew until cancelled in your Apple ID subscriptions) or lifetime, a one-time purchase that never renews. Apple handles the purchase, and with Universal Purchase one Premium unlocks PETO on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Premium also covers things like creating family sharing, medication routines and the vet PDF report. Core reminders, logging and the living pet below stay free.

Meet the living pet: pet it, watch it sleep, spot the muddy paws

Outfits are the seasonal part. The everyday part is free: on Home and on the pet page, your pixel pet is "alive".

  • Press and hold to pet it. A dog wags, a cat or small furry pet purrs, birds and fish flutter, and reptiles nuzzle. You feel it through haptics, and it's mirrored on Apple Watch.
  • Bedtime and wake-up. At night your pet sleeps in a nightcap under a moon. The first time you look each morning, it yawns awake with a sun.
  • Muddy paws. Right after you log a walk, your pet wanders about on muddy paws for a while. Tracking walks on your wrist? See Apple Watch walks.

With Reduce Motion turned on, the looks stay but the movement stops. A pet in memorial mode is drawn still.

PETO tip

Petting the pixel pet is a nice little pause, but the real one probably wants it more. The free Attention routine reminds you to make time for cuddles, and with pet voice reminders it can even arrive in your pet's own bark or meow.

Fold fun on iPhone Duo, iPad and Mac

PETO is built for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and it has a little trick for big screen changes. On a foldable iPhone (iPhone Duo), in iPad split view or when you maximise a Mac window, a big one-step size change plays a short animation: pixel covers swing open and your pets hop out with hearts. Folding back squashes the app and puffs out paw prints.

It's decorative only, so your routines and reminders stay as they are, and Reduce Motion turns it off.

Dressing up your real pet? Keep it short, comfortable and supervised

A pixel hat costs your pet nothing. A real costume is different, and animal-welfare charities are cautious about it. The PDSA advises avoiding clothes that aren't designed to keep a pet warm or safe, because outfits can get in the way of going to the toilet or grooming and can make a pet too hot. The ASPCA recommends not putting a dog or cat in a costume unless you know they love it.

If you still want a festive photo of your real pet:

  • Choose a costume that doesn't limit movement, sight, breathing or the ability to bark or meow, with no small, dangling or easily chewed-off pieces (ASPCA).
  • Let your pet try it on before the big day, and keep it on only briefly, for example for a photo (ASPCA, RSPCA Australia).
  • Watch for stress. RSPCA Australia mentions panting, lip licking and showing the whites of the eyes; the PDSA adds yawning in dogs and over-grooming, aggression or extra noise in cats. If your pet tries to get it off, take it off.
  • Supervise the whole time and never leave a pet in a costume unattended (RSPCA Australia).
  • Cats generally don't enjoy being dressed up. A festive bandana for a moment, or no costume at all, is fine (RSPCA Australia, ASPCA).

If your pet seems stressed or unwell afterwards, ask your vet.

Questions people ask

When can I dress my pet up for Halloween, New Year or Bayram in PETO?

Any time. PETO features Halloween from 1 October to 7 November, New Year from 1 December to 15 January and Bayram from a week before Ramazan Bayramı and Kurban Bayramı, with dates from the Umm al-Qura calendar. Released outfits stay wearable all year.

Are PETO's seasonal outfits free?

Trying them on is free for everyone. Keeping an outfit on your pet is part of optional PETO Premium, sold as monthly, yearly or lifetime.

How do I pet my pixel pet in PETO?

Press and hold your pet on Home or on its pet page. A dog wags, a cat or small furry pet purrs, birds and fish flutter and reptiles nuzzle, with haptics that are mirrored on Apple Watch. It's free.

Why is my pet asleep in PETO?

At night your pixel pet sleeps in a nightcap under a moon, and the first time you look each morning it yawns awake with a sun. It's part of the free living pet. With Reduce Motion on, the looks stay but the movement stops.

Is it safe to dress up my real pet for Halloween?

Animal-welfare charities are cautious. The ASPCA suggests a costume only if you know your pet loves it, and RSPCA Australia says to keep it brief, supervise closely and never leave a pet in a costume unattended. Watch for stress such as panting or lip licking, and ask your vet if you're unsure.
