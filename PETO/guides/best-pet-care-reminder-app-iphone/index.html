How to choose a pet-care reminder app for iPhone: an 8-point checklist

By Gül Eda Aydemir · Updated November 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Reading font
Quick answer

A good pet-care reminder app for iPhone delivers reminders reliably, even offline, logs care with one tap, handles several pets and species, lets a family share care without double feeding, keeps a health record with vet reminders, respects your privacy, works with Apple Watch and Siri, and is priced fairly. PETO answers these points with on-device reminders, one-tap logging, 13 species, iCloud family sharing and no account, ads or tracking. Its core reminders and logging are free; optional Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) covers extras such as the medication routine, the vet PDF and creating a shared household.

Key points

  • Reminders scheduled by iOS on the device don't depend on a server or a signal.
  • One-tap logging, also from the notification, is what keeps a routine going.
  • Shared care only works if everyone can see who already fed or walked the pet.
  • Check whether an app needs an account, shows ads or collects data linked to you.
  • Know what is free, what is paid, and whether you can pay once instead of subscribing.

What makes a good pet-care reminder app for iPhone?

A pet-care reminder app has one job: making sure meals, walks, play and medicine happen on time, and only once, without you keeping it all in your head. Almost any app can send a notification. The differences are in the details: whether reminders arrive when you're offline, how fast you can mark something done, and whether two people end up feeding the cat twice.

Use this checklist when you compare apps; below, each point explains what to look for and how PETO answers it, gaps included.

Do the reminders work offline and on time?

Reminders are the heart of a care app, so check how they are delivered. Reminders that iOS schedules locally on your iPhone don't depend on a company's server, or on having a signal at feeding time.

PETO: reminders are local notifications that iOS schedules from your routines; PETO has no server. A rolling schedule covers the next three days and is rebuilt whenever you open the app or change something. Each routine has its own “Remind me” setting: at the time, 10 minutes before or 30 minutes before.

Can you log feeding or a walk with one tap?

If marking a job as done takes five taps, people stop doing it, and then the app can't tell anyone what's done. Look for logging straight from the notification and a quick button in the app.

PETO: big FEED, WALK, PLAY and CUDDLE buttons on each pet's page, Quick Actions on Home, and “Fed ✓”, “Snooze 15 min” and “Skip” right on the notification. Logging cancels that reminder and is optional; reminders work either way.

Does it handle several pets and different species?

Plenty of homes have more than one animal, and not all of them are dogs and cats. Check that each pet gets its own schedule and that the app's suggestions fit the animals you actually have.

PETO: multiple pets, each with its own routines, logs, appointments and health record. It supports 13 species, from dogs, cats and rabbits to birds, fish, turtles, lizards, snakes and “other”. During onboarding it suggests routines per species, such as feeding, a walk, playtime and attention for a dog, or feeding and aquarium care for a fish; they are only suggestions. Cats also get free litter, water and toilet tracking, and fish and turtles a tank-cleaning routine: see the cat litter and water guide and the aquarium cleaning guide.

Can the whole family share care without double feeding?

“Did anyone feed the cat?” is exactly the question a shared app should answer. Look for sharing where everyone sees who did what, ideally without yet another account.

PETO: Family sharing keeps one household on your own iCloud through Apple's CloudKit sharing. Everyone sees who fed, walked or played, with notifications like “Eda fed Buddy 🐾 — already done, no need to do it again.” Creating a household needs Premium; joining is free by opening the invitation link on an iPhone. PETO can't read the shared data. See the family sharing guide.

Does it keep a health record and vet reminders?

Care is more than meals. A useful app keeps vaccines, weight and vet visits next to the daily routine, and reminds you before a booster or an appointment.

PETO: each pet has a free health record with vaccines and preventives (with booster reminders), a weight log with a trend chart, and vet visits. Vet appointments with reminders are free too. The medication routine and an A4 vet report (PDF) are Premium. More in the health record guide.

Does it need an account, show ads or track you?

A pet-care app learns a lot about your days: when you're home, when you go out, who lives with you. Check the App Store privacy label, where Apple separates data used to track you, data linked to you, data not linked to you and data not collected, and ask whether you really need to sign up.

PETO: no PETO account, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no server of its own. Profiles, routines, logs and health records are stored on your device, and walks go to Apple Health only if you allow it. PETO does count anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), such as which screens and features are used, but never pet names, notes, photos or health records; its privacy label lists them as data not linked to you, used for analytics. You can turn them off in Settings → Data → “Share anonymous usage stats”. Pet data is never sold. Details are on the privacy page.

Does it work with Apple Watch and Siri?

You often need to log something when your hands are full: a lead in one hand, a food bowl in the other. Watch and voice support let you log without unlocking your phone.

PETO: the Apple Watch app (watchOS 10 or later) lists pets by urgency, logs Feed, Walk, Play, Cuddle or Meds with one tap, tracks walks as a HealthKit walking workout and offers complications for the most urgent pet. Reminders reach the wrist with their buttons, and on iPhone, Siri understands phrases like “I fed Buddy in PETO”. See the Apple Watch walks guide.

Is the pricing fair: free core, subscription or one-time?

Check what the free version covers; ideally reminders and logging aren't behind a paywall. Some people like a subscription, others prefer to pay once. Subscriptions bought through Apple can be cancelled in Settings, under your name and then Subscriptions, as Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription explains.

PETO: free to download, no ads. Reminders, logging, vet appointments, weekly stats and the health record are free. PETO Premium is optional: monthly or yearly (auto-renewing until you cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions) or lifetime, a one-time purchase that never renews. It adds extras such as the medication routine, trends, the vet PDF, creating Family sharing, more avatars and themes, and history export. Premium keeps working offline once verified, and one purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Cat care, aquarium care, pet voices and memorial mode are free extras.

What doesn't PETO do?

An honest checklist includes the gaps. PETO may not suit you if:

  • Someone at home uses Android. There's no Android version. PETO is built for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with an Apple Watch app, and household invitations are opened on an iPhone.
  • You want the medication routine, the vet PDF or your own shared household for free. Those are Premium; joining someone else's household is free.
  • You'd mostly use it on a Mac. PETO is built for Mac too, but Live Activities, the Screen Time nudge and alternate app icons aren't available there.
  • You want health advice. Labels like “Needs a walk” are friendly signals from your schedule and logs, not medical assessments. Ask your vet.
  • You like streak counters. PETO skips streak pressure on purpose: gentle reminders instead of guilt.

Questions people ask

What should I look for in a pet care reminder app?

Reliable reminders (ideally scheduled on the device so they work offline), one-tap logging, support for several pets and species, family sharing that shows who already did what, a health record with vet reminders, clear privacy with no account, ads or tracking, Apple Watch and Siri support, and a pricing model you are comfortable with.

Is there a pet care reminder app with no ads and no account?

Yes. PETO has no ads and no PETO account. It has no server of its own and stores your pet data on your device; optional Family sharing syncs through your own iCloud. Anonymous usage statistics can be turned off in Settings → Data.

Is PETO available in Spanish?

Yes. PETO's own text is in 13 languages, including Spanish (Spain and Mexico), English and Turkish. You can switch the language inside the app and it applies instantly. Its website, including this guide, is also available in Spanish.

Is PETO a subscription or a one-time purchase?

Neither is required. PETO is free to download, with free reminders and logging. Optional PETO Premium is offered monthly or yearly (auto-renewing until cancelled) or as a lifetime one-time purchase that never renews.
