Common questions

Quick answers

How do I contact Solora support?

Email solostaseraofficial@gmail.com with your question, your device model and iOS version. In the app, Settings → Contact support opens this page. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot when reporting a bug.

Where did my completed or deleted items go?

They're in the Archive. Since version 3.0, completed tasks move to the Archive, and deleting an item moves it there too. Open Settings → Data → Archive to restore an item or delete it permanently.

Is my data backed up or synced between devices?

Solora stores everything on your device, with no account and no server. Your data is included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), but there's no cloud sync between devices and no export. If you delete the app, its data is erased from that device. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Solora, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still doesn't restore, email me and I'll help.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not inside the app:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Solora and tap Cancel Subscription.

Premium stays active until the end of the current billing period. The Lifetime purchase isn't a subscription, so there's nothing to cancel.

How do refunds work?

All purchases go through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find Solora and request a refund. If you bought by mistake or hit a problem, email me first and I'll do my best to help.

Why does the assistant say Apple Intelligence isn't available?

Solora's assistant runs on Apple Intelligence on your device, so it needs iOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone or iPad (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or later, or an iPad with M1 or later; see Apple's requirements). The message tells you what to do:

  • “Apple Intelligence isn't supported on this device.” This device can't run Apple Intelligence. Everything else in Solora still works.
  • “Turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings to use Solora's assistant.” Open Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri and turn it on.
  • “Apple Intelligence is getting ready. Try again shortly.” The model is still downloading. Stay on Wi-Fi and power, then try again.
  • “Update to the latest iOS to use Solora's on-device assistant.” Update to iOS 26 or later.

Without Apple Intelligence, the assistant can still turn short requests into tasks, habits or projects and suggest a basic plan.

Does Solora work offline?

Yes. Solora has no server, so planning works without internet, and so does the assistant on supported devices because it runs on the device. You only need a connection to download the app or buy and restore Premium.

Notifications and reminders aren't showing up. What should I check?

Open the Settings app → Solora → Notifications and make sure they're allowed. Check that a Focus mode isn't silencing them, and that reminders are turned on in Solora's own Settings → Notifications and the task or habit has a reminder time.

How do I add Solora widgets or use Siri?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, then search for Solora. There are 11 widgets, including the interactive “Check in” habit widget; some are Premium. For Siri or Shortcuts, say “Quick add to Solora” and it sorts what you say into a task, habit or project.

Does Solora read or change my calendar?

Only if you connect it, in Settings → Calendar. Solora then reads your events on the device to find free time when it plans. It adds events only when you turn on “Add tasks to calendar” and choose to schedule the steps of an AI project breakdown. You can remove access any time in the iOS Settings app.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Solora runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18.6 or later; the AI assistant needs iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence. It's available in English, Turkish, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Simplified Chinese. Switch languages in Settings → Language. To see the animated walkthrough again, open Settings → Replay Basics Guide; Learn the basics explains each concept.
