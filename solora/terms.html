Solora requires iOS or iPadOS 18.6 or later. The AI assistant additionally requires iOS 26 or later on an Apple Intelligence–capable device with Apple Intelligence turned on. You're responsible for having a compatible device and operating system. Some features depend on your device's capabilities and may not be available on all hardware.

2. No account, your content

Solora does not require you to create an account. The content you create — tasks, habits, projects, notes, roles and assistant conversations — is stored on your device. Because there is no account and no server holding your content, you are responsible for your data. Deleted and completed items go to the in-app Archive, where you can restore them. If you delete an item permanently from the Archive, or uninstall the app, we cannot recover that content. Solora doesn't sync between devices; your normal device backup is the only copy outside the app.

4. AI assistant

Solora includes an optional assistant powered by Apple's on-device foundation models (Apple Intelligence). The assistant generates suggestions to help you plan, break down projects and adjust your day; new items it suggests appear as proposals you can edit before saving. It requires iOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence–capable device, and may be unavailable on some hardware or in some languages and regions.

AI-generated output can be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for your situation. The assistant is a planning aid only — it does not provide professional, medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Use your own judgment and verify anything important.
