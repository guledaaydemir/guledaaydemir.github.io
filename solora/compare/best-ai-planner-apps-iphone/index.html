The best AI planner apps for iPhone, compared

"AI planner" can mean anything from a chat box to an engine that rebuilds your calendar. Here's what each app's AI really does, what it costs, and who it's for.

GABy Gül Eda Aydemir, indie iOS developer·Updated 3 Nov 2026·5 min read

Quick answer

There's no single best one. Choose Motion or Akiflow for scheduling a heavy work calendar from a computer, Structured for AI that drafts a visual day and replans missed tasks, Tiimo for turning a brain dump into a calm day, and TickTick for AI inside a free all-round task app. Choose Solora if you want the AI to run entirely on your iPhone, with no account and nothing sent to a server. It needs iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence device.

Which AI planner is best for a busy work calendar?

Motion and Akiflow are built for that, and both make the most sense if you live at a computer. Motion's listing is direct about it: it "is best used on a computer" and the mobile app is a companion.

They connect to your work calendars and, in Akiflow's case, thousands of tools. Solora doesn't compete here. It has no team features or work integrations.

Which AI planner is best for a visual day?

Structured and Tiimo. Both lay out your day visually, and both use AI to draft it.

Structured's AI planning and replanning are in Pro, while its timeline is free. Tiimo's AI Co-Planner is aimed at people who find executive function hard, and it's designed with neurodivergent users in mind.

Which AI planner keeps your data private?

Solora is the only app on this list with the label Data Not Collected. Its assistant uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models, and voice input is transcribed on the device too.

A planner knows a lot about you: your health tasks, family plans, money worries. Solora has no account and no server, so that information stays on your device, and the assistant works without internet on supported devices.

That choice has a cost. An on-device model is smaller than the big cloud models, and it needs iOS 26 on a supported device such as an iPhone 15 Pro or iPhone 16 or later. On an older iPhone you still get roles, the Big 3, habits and projects, but not the assistant.

What does Solora's AI do for free?

The free plan includes 5 assistant chats a day and 3 AI project breakdowns a month. You can type something like "I need to renew my passport, book the vet and start running" and get editable proposals you can undo.

Premium removes those limits and adds calendar-aware day planning, one-tap plans for prioritizing, rescheduling and lighter days, assistant memory for preferences you tell it, and an AI-written weekly report.

What can't Solora do?

Solora is a personal planner for one person on iPhone and iPad, and it has real gaps.

  • No cloud sync between devices, no web app and no data export. Your data stays on the device and in its normal backup.
  • No shared or team lists, and no Apple Watch app or native Mac app.
  • The AI assistant needs iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence device (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or later, or an M1 iPad or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything else works without it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free AI planner app for iPhone?

Solora has a free plan with 5 on-device assistant chats a day and needs no account, on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence. TickTick is free and lists an AI Assistant. Structured is free, but its AI planning needs Pro.

Is there an AI planner that works offline?

Yes. Solora's assistant runs on the device with Apple Intelligence, so it works without internet on supported devices. The rest of the app also works offline because it has no server.

Which AI planner doesn't send my data to a server?

Solora runs its AI on the device using Apple's Foundation Models, has no account and shows Data Not Collected on the App Store. Check the listing and privacy policy of any other app, since most don't state this in their listing.

Is Motion worth it on iPhone?

Only if you also use it on a computer. Motion's App Store listing says the mobile app is a companion and not a standalone app, and it requires a subscription, listed at $44.99 a month or $294.99 a year.

Does Solora's assistant work on every iPhone?

No. It needs iOS 26 or later on an Apple Intelligence device, such as iPhone 15 Pro or iPhone 16 and later, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Every other feature works from iOS 18.6.
