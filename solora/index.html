Not another to-do list

What is Solora?

Solora: AI Life Planner is a free planner for iPhone and iPad by independent developer Gül Eda Aydemir. You organize tasks, habits and projects by the roles you live (parent, student, creator, professional…), choose a short daily focus list called the Big 3, and get help from an Apple Intelligence assistant that runs on your device. There's no account and no server: the App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

How it works

Three steps to a calmer week

Set up your roles once. After that, it's a quick daily pick and a short weekly look back.

  1. 1

    Name the roles you live

    Pick from 23 role templates (Professional, Parent, Student, Creator, Athlete…) or make your own. Each role gets a color, an icon and a small weekly target.

  2. 2

    Pick today's Big 3

    Choose the three things that would make today a good day (or 1, or 5). Anything unfinished carries over to tomorrow, and overdue tasks keep a “Late” tag instead of quietly disappearing.

  3. 3

    Review the week, rebalance

    The Weekly Review shows your score, habit consistency and how each role did against its target, so you can see which role could use more attention next week.

The method

What is the Big 3 method?

The Big 3 means choosing the three most important things for today and doing them before anything else. Not thirty. Three. When everything is a priority, nothing is, so you commit to the few that matter and let the rest wait in one place.

  • Three is achievable. Even a chaotic day usually has room for three wins, so a busy day can still end with real progress.
  • Choosing is the work. Deciding what matters most is harder, and more valuable, than doing busywork.
  • Roles keep it balanced. Each role has a weekly target for finished tasks, and your Weekly Review shows which role you've neglected, so tomorrow's Big 3 can include it.
  • Nothing is lost. Unfinished Big 3 items carry over to tomorrow, marked as carried over, instead of vanishing into a backlog.
  • Your number, your call. Prefer one big thing, or five small ones? Choose 1, 3 or 5 daily priorities during setup.

Want the full method, with examples and how it compares to MIT, 1-3-5 and Ivy Lee? Read the Big 3 method guide.

What Solora does for you

One calm home for your whole life

Not a longer list of features. Fewer decisions, kinder bad days, and a clear picture of what matters.

Roles

Every part of your life gets its own space

Work stops swallowing everything else. Each role holds its own tasks, habits and projects, with a small weekly target.

  • 23 role templates across work, study, content, family, home, health and growth, plus your own custom roles
  • A weekly target per role, for example Parent 3, Health 3, Work 5. In Solora 3.0 it counts the tasks you complete in that role; habits and project steps don't count toward it yet
  • See which role is falling behind in the Weekly Review's Role Balance, or ask the assistant “Which role am I neglecting?”

Habits

Habits that survive real life

A streak shouldn't die because you got sick. Solora plans for the bad days too.

  • Simple, count, timer and step-by-step routine habits, with 52 templates to start from
  • An easier version for low-energy days: a 5-minute stretch instead of a 30-minute workout (set it on the habit; log it in one tap from the Premium Light Mode widget)
  • Strict, flexible or shielded streaks: protect a day with a reason like sick, travel or rest
  • Heatmap, history and charts for every habit, free

On-device AI

A private assistant that does the busywork

Type or say what's on your mind. Solora turns it into tasks, habits and projects as cards you can edit before anything is saved, and you can undo.

  • Create several items from one message, plan your day, prioritize, or lighten the load when you're overwhelmed
  • Break a big goal into steps with time estimates (3 a month on the free plan)
  • Voice input is transcribed on your device, and the assistant replies in the language you write in

The assistant runs on Apple Intelligence, on your device. It needs iOS 26 or later and an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone or iPad (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or later, or an iPad with M1 or later; see Apple's list) with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything else in Solora works on iOS 18.6 and later.

Insights

Questions

Solora FAQ

What is Solora: AI Life Planner?

Solora: AI Life Planner is a free planner for iPhone and iPad by independent developer Gül Eda Aydemir. You organize tasks, habits and projects by the roles you live, pick a short daily focus list called the Big 3, and get help from an AI assistant that runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. It needs no account.

What is the Big 3 method, and how does Solora use it?

The Big 3 means choosing the three outcomes that would make today a success and doing them before anything else. In Solora you choose 1, 3 or 5 daily priorities during setup, pick them from your tasks, habits and projects, and unfinished ones carry over to tomorrow.

How do I balance work, family and time for myself?

Give each role a small weekly target instead of chasing equal hours, then check once a week which role got neglected. Solora's Weekly Review shows completed tasks vs target for every role (habits don't count toward the target in Solora 3.0), and you can ask the assistant “Which role am I neglecting?”, so tomorrow's Big 3 can include it. The role-based planning guide walks through it.

Is Solora free? What does Premium add?

Yes, Solora is free to download and use. The free plan includes 4 roles, 15 active tasks, 20 habits, 5 projects, 5 assistant chats a day and 3 AI project breakdowns a month. Premium (monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime; see the current price on the App Store) removes those limits and adds calendar-aware AI day planning, one-tap assistant plans (prioritize, reschedule, lighter day), assistant memory for your preferences, an AI weekly report, premium widgets and widget themes.

Is there an option without a subscription?

Yes. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no subscription; see the current price on the App Store. The monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until you cancel them in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Does Solora's AI send my data to the cloud?

No. The assistant runs on Apple's on-device models (Apple Intelligence), and voice input is transcribed on the device. Solora has no account, no server and no analytics or advertising SDKs, and its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Does Solora work without Apple Intelligence?

Yes. Roles, the Big 3, tasks, habits, projects, rituals, Insights and widgets work on any iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.6 or later. The AI assistant needs iOS 26 and an Apple Intelligence–capable device (such as iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 or later, or an iPad with M1 or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, the assistant can still turn simple requests into items and suggest a basic plan.

Does Solora work on iPad, and does it sync between devices?

Solora runs on both iPhone and iPad. There's no cloud sync, so each device keeps its own data, which is included in that device's normal backup. There's no Apple Watch or Mac app and no data export.

Does Solora work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored on your device and there's no server, so planning works without internet, and so does the assistant on supported devices. You only need a connection to download the app or buy Premium.

What happens on a bad or low-energy day?

Solora is built for imperfect days. The Morning Kickoff starts with an energy check-in, habits can have an easier version (a 5-minute stretch instead of a workout, logged in one tap from the Premium Light Mode widget), streaks can be shielded with a reason like sick or travel, and when a task slips Solora asks what got in the way and helps you split it, shrink it or move it. It's a planner, not medical advice: if you feel exhausted for weeks, talk to a doctor. More ideas in what to do on a low-energy day.

Which languages does Solora support?

Six: English, Turkish, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Simplified Chinese. Solora follows your device language, you can switch it in Settings → Language, and the assistant replies in the language you write in.

Does Solora have widgets and Siri shortcuts?

Yes. There are 11 Home Screen widgets, including Big 3, Today's Focus, Add to Solora, Streak and the interactive Check in widget, where you can complete or skip a habit right from the Home Screen. Some widgets are Premium, including the full Light Mode widget, which shows your smallest next step and logs a habit's easier version in one tap. With Siri or Shortcuts, say “Quick add to Solora” and it sorts what you say into a task, habit or project.
