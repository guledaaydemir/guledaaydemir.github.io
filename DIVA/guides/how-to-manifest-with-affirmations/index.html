How to Manifest with Affirmations (The Supermodel Method)

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

To manifest with affirmations, pick one specific area of life (your 'era'), write 5 to 10 short, present-tense sentences that feel believable, listen to or say them every day at the same moment, and pair them with one small real-world action. Affirmations work on focus and mood; the action is what moves things.

Babe. Sit down. We need to talk about manifesting, because the internet has made it either a magic spell or a scam, and it's neither. It's a habit. A very glamorous habit, if you do it my way.

What does manifesting with affirmations actually mean?

Manifesting is deciding what you want, saying it like it's already true, and then behaving like the girl who has it. Affirmations are the saying part: short, first-person, present-tense sentences you repeat until they stop feeling like a costume and start feeling like your skin.

The honest version: affirmations don't move money into your account. They move your attention. And attention decides what you notice, what you say yes to and how you walk into a room.

The supermodel method, in 4 steps

  1. Pick one era. Not twelve. One. Confidence, money, love, dream home, career. The girl who wants everything at once manifests a headache.
  2. Write it in the present tense. "I will be confident" is a waiting room. "I walk into a room and feel at home in myself" is the room.
  3. Make it believable-ish. If "I am a billionaire" makes your brain laugh, try "I am calm when I think about money." Stretch, don't snap.
  4. Repeat it at the same moment every day. Coffee, commute, skincare, bedtime. Attach it to something you already do and it sticks.

Why listening beats reading off a sticky note

Reading affirmations is fine. Hearing them is a whole different movie. A warm voice in your headphones, a soft soundscape underneath, ten minutes where nobody needs anything from you. That's why I built DIVA as CDs: you press play and the ritual does itself.

In DIVA every era is a CD with an intro, 20-something affirmations and an outro. You pick the voice (Velvet Whisper is my bedtime girl, Pink Muse is my getting-ready girl) and the length: 10, 20 or 60 minutes.

The part nobody puts on TikTok: one tiny action

After your session, do one small thing your dream girl would do. Money era? Check one number in your banking app with curiosity, not fear. Confidence era? Send the email. Dream home era? Save one photo to your vision board. Small. Daily. Iconic.

Common manifesting mistakes

  • Affirmations you secretly hate. If a line feels cringe, rewrite it. Your words, your era.
  • Checking for results every hour. You're planting, not microwaving.
  • Using it to skip feelings. Affirmations sit next to real emotions; they don't replace them. If you're struggling, talk to someone you trust or a professional.

Quick questions

How long does it take to manifest with affirmations?

There's no fixed timeline, and anyone who gives you one is selling something. Most people notice a shift in mood and focus within a couple of weeks of daily practice. Real-world changes come from what you do with that focus.

Should affirmations be in the present tense?

Yes. Present-tense, first-person lines ('I am', 'I have', 'I choose') feel like a description of who you are now, which is easier to step into than a someday promise.

Is it better to say affirmations or listen to them?

Both work. Listening is easier to keep up because it needs no effort: put on headphones and press play. Many people listen and repeat the lines in their head.
