Rich Girl Affirmations: Money Affirmations That Don't Feel Cringe

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

Money affirmations work best when they target how you feel about money, not a magic number: lines like 'I am calm when I think about money' and 'I have options, I have taste, I have time'. Use them daily alongside one small money action. They support a calmer money mindset; they are not financial advice.

Let's talk about money, honey. Not in a finance-bro way. In a "I want to open my banking app without needing to lie down" way.

What is a "rich girl" affirmation?

Rich girl energy isn't about pretending you're a billionaire. It's options, taste and time. It's calm. Rich girls don't panic-refresh their balance; they look at the numbers like a menu. So the best money affirmations are about the feeling, not the figure.

Money affirmations to steal

  • "I am calm when I think about money."
  • "I have options. I have taste. I have time."
  • "Money supports the life I choose to create."
  • "I enjoy beautiful things without guilt."
  • "I look at my numbers with curiosity, not fear."
  • "I make smart financial decisions."
  • "I spend with joy, and I save with pride."

The first five are from Rich Girl Frequency (free in DIVA); the last two are from Million Dollar Energy (PRO).

Why believable beats huge

If you say "I have a million dollars" and your brain goes ma'am, you have $43, the affirmation loses. Start where your nervous system can follow: calm, curiosity, choices. Then stretch. "I am building wealth with confidence and care" is a sentence you can grow into.

Pair it with one money move

After your session, do one tiny thing: check one account, cancel one subscription you forgot, move a little into savings, write down one price you'd charge for your skills. The affirmation sets the mood; the move makes the movie.

The honest bit

DIVA is a mindset ritual, not financial advice, and no CD will pick stocks for you. If you need real money help, talk to a qualified advisor. Then go listen to your CD like the CEO you are.

Quick questions

Do money affirmations work?

They can help you feel calmer and more intentional about money, which makes it easier to take practical steps. They don't create money on their own and they aren't financial advice.

What is the best money affirmation?

One you can actually believe today. 'I am calm when I think about money' is a great starting point because it changes how you approach your finances.

What does 'rich girl frequency' mean?

It's a playful way to describe feeling at ease with money: having options, taste and time, and enjoying beautiful things without guilt. It's also the name of a free DIVA CD.
