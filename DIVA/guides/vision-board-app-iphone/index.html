✦ The quick answer, babe

To make a vision board on iPhone, write down 3 to 5 specific dreams, pick one photo for each that makes you feel it, put them together in one place you'll see daily, and review progress weekly. DIVA's Wishlist does this in one app: dreams, photos, progress, milestones, a vision board and a daily reminder, all stored privately on your phone.

Glue sticks and magazine cut-outs are cute, but your vision board should live where you actually look 96 times a day: your phone. Here's how to make one that doesn't end up behind the wardrobe.

How to make a vision board on iPhone, step by step

  1. Write 3 to 5 dreams. Specific beats vague. Not "travel more" but "Paris, window seat, spring."
  2. Pick one photo per dream. One that gives you a tiny jolt. Your own photos hit harder than Pinterest.
  3. Give each dream a next step. A dream without a step is a screensaver.
  4. Put it where you'll see it daily. A reminder, a widget, a ritual.
  5. Review weekly. Move the progress bar, celebrate the wins, edit the dreams that changed.

How the DIVA Wishlist works

I built the Wishlist because my dreams were scattered across Notes, screenshots and a journal I lost in 2019. In DIVA:

  • Write each dream with a category (travel, home, career, love, money and more) and an optional note.
  • Add photos from your library or snap one with the camera to build your vision board.
  • Track progress and milestones, then tap It's Already Mine ♡ and get a card to save.
  • Link a dream to a CD, so your dream home dream plays Penthouse State of Mind.
  • Turn on the daily vision reminder to keep your dream in sight.

Free users can keep up to 5 dreams on their way at once (achieved ones don't count) with the grid board; PRO unlocks unlimited dreams and every board layout.

Quick questions

What is the best way to make a vision board on iPhone?

Use an app that keeps dreams, photos and progress together and reminds you daily. DIVA's Wishlist combines a vision board with progress tracking and matching affirmation CDs.

How many goals should be on a vision board?

Three to five is the sweet spot: enough to inspire you, few enough to actually focus on.

Are digital vision boards as good as paper ones?

They can be better, because you see them more often. What matters is looking at it daily and taking small steps.
