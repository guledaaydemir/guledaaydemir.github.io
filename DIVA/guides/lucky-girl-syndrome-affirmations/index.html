Lucky Girl Syndrome: What It Is & Affirmations to Try

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

Lucky girl syndrome is the trend of repeating that you're lucky and that everything works out for you, so you start expecting and noticing good things. Try affirmations like 'Everything is working out for me' and 'I am always in the right place at the right time', and keep taking real chances: luck loves a girl who shows up.

"I'm so lucky, everything always works out for me." If you said that out loud just now, congratulations, you're officially in your lucky girl era. Let's make it chic and not delusional.

What is lucky girl syndrome?

It's a manifesting trend where you decide you're a lucky person and repeat it until you believe it. Then you start noticing the parking spot, the reply, the upgrade. Part of the charm is psychology: when you expect good things, you notice more of them, and you're more likely to say yes when they show up.

Lucky girl affirmations

  • "Everything is working out for me."
  • "Things are falling into place."
  • "I am always in the right place at the right time."
  • "Good news finds me easily."
  • "I notice beautiful opportunities everywhere."
  • "I handle every challenge with confidence."

Those are from Everything Works Out For Me, a free DIVA CD. Put it on before an interview, a first date or a Monday.

Where lucky girl syndrome goes wrong

  • Expecting luck to do your homework. Lucky girls still apply, send, ask and show up. They just do it like they expect a yes.
  • Blaming yourself for bad days. Rough days happen to lucky girls too. They're weather, not a verdict.
  • Ignoring real problems. Affirmations sit beside real life, not instead of it.

A 3-day lucky girl challenge

  1. Every morning, listen to Everything Works Out For Me for 10 minutes.
  2. Write down three lucky things before bed. Tiny counts. Especially tiny.
  3. Say yes to one small opportunity each day.

By day three you'll be the girl who says "of course it worked out" with a hair flip.

Quick questions

Is lucky girl syndrome real?

It isn't a medical syndrome; it's a mindset trend. The useful part is that expecting good things can make you more likely to notice and act on opportunities.

What do you say for lucky girl syndrome?

Short, confident lines like 'Everything is working out for me', 'I am so lucky' and 'Good news finds me easily', repeated daily.

Is lucky girl syndrome the same as manifesting?

It's a flavour of manifesting focused on feeling lucky in general rather than on one specific goal.
