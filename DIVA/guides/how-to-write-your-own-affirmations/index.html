How to Write Your Own Affirmations (That Sound Like You)

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

To write your own affirmations, use first person and present tense ('I am', 'I have', 'I choose'), keep each line short and specific, make it believable enough that you don't flinch, phrase it positively (what you want, not what you fear) and read it out loud: if it sounds like you, keep it.

Store-bought affirmations are fine. Custom ones are couture. Here's my formula for writing lines that actually feel like yours.

The 5-step affirmation formula

  1. First person. Start with I or My. You're the main character, not a narrator.
  2. Present tense. "I am", not "I will be". Future tense keeps the dream in the future.
  3. Short and specific. Under 12 words. "My mornings are soft and unhurried" beats a paragraph.
  4. Believable-ish. Stretch it, don't snap it. If your brain argues, soften it: "I am learning to…" is allowed.
  5. Positive phrasing. "I am calm" instead of "I am not anxious". The brain hears the word you repeat.

Before and after

  • "I won't be broke anymore" → "I make money decisions with a calm, clear mind."
  • "I hope people like me" → "I am the kind of person people feel lucky to know."
  • "I need to stop being lazy" → "I move my body because I love it."

Read it out loud

This is the test. Say it in your normal voice. If you'd cringe saying it to your best friend, rewrite it. If you'd text it to her with three sparkle emojis, it's a keeper.

Quick questions

What makes a good affirmation?

First person, present tense, short, specific, positively phrased and believable enough that you don't instantly argue with it.

Should affirmations start with 'I am'?

'I am' is a classic, but 'I have', 'I choose', 'My' and 'I am learning to' work beautifully too.

Can I record my own affirmations in DIVA?

DIVA doesn't record your voice. You can build a CD from the app's affirmations, and with DIVA PRO write your own lines, which DIVA voices on your iPhone.
