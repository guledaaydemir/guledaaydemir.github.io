A 10-Minute 'That Girl' Morning Affirmations Routine

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

A good morning affirmation routine takes 5 to 10 minutes: before checking your phone, take three slow breaths, set one intention for the day, then listen to or say 5 to 8 present-tense affirmations. Rotating a theme for each weekday (confidence, calm focus, abundance, self-love) keeps it fresh.

Raise your hand if the first thing you see every morning is 47 notifications and someone's engagement post. Same. That's why the first ten minutes are sacred now. Here's the routine I actually do (in a robe, obviously).

The 10-minute routine

  1. Minute 0: don't touch your phone. Okay, touch it once, to press play.
  2. Minutes 1–2: three slow breaths. In through the nose, long exhale. Shoulders down from your ears, diva.
  3. Minute 3: one intention. One sentence for today. "Today I move slowly and arrive on time."
  4. Minutes 4–9: affirmations. Listen, and repeat each line in your head. Feel it like a perfume you're trying on.
  5. Minute 10: one tiny action. Water, sunlight, make the bed. Main characters hydrate.

A theme for every day of the week

Doing the same lines every day gets stale fast. This is the rotation inside DIVA's Morning Affirmations ritual:

  • Monday: A fresh start
  • Tuesday: Confidence
  • Wednesday: Calm focus
  • Thursday: Abundance
  • Friday: Joy and glow
  • Saturday: Self-love
  • Sunday: Gratitude

Morning affirmations that don't sound like a poster

  • "Good morning to me."
  • "Today has my name on it."
  • "I woke up as myself, and that is excellent news."
  • "I choose my mood before I check my phone."
  • "My energy is fresh and all mine."

Those are from Wake Up, Icon, a free DIVA CD made for exactly this.

How to do it in DIVA

Open My CDs and tap Morning Affirmations at the top. Pick 5 or 10 minutes (free) or 20 (PRO). Turn on the morning reminder in Settings and it opens the ritual straight away, already on today's theme. Lazy-girl approved.

Quick questions

What is the best time to say morning affirmations?

Right after you wake up and before you check your phone, when your mind is quiet and nothing has set the mood for you yet.

How many affirmations should I say in the morning?

Five to eight is plenty. Fewer lines you actually feel beat fifty you rush through.

Can I do a morning affirmation routine in 5 minutes?

Yes. Three breaths, one intention and a short affirmation session. DIVA's Morning ritual has a free 5-minute version.
