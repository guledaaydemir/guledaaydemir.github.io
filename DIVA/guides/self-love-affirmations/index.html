Self-Love & Love Affirmations: Attract, Don't Chase

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

Self-love affirmations remind you that your worth isn't up for discussion: 'I am more than enough, even on quiet days', 'My boundaries are elegant and strong'. Love affirmations then build on that: 'I don't chase. I attract.' Start with worth, then love. A girl who knows her value attracts people who see it.

Babe, before we manifest the man, the woman or the situationship upgrade, we're manifesting you. Self-love first. Always. Then love looks good on you.

Self-love affirmations

  • "I am more than enough, even on quiet days."
  • "My worth is not up for discussion."
  • "My boundaries are elegant and strong."
  • "I say no, and I stay kind."
  • "I stay where I am valued."

Those are from I'm the Prize, my CD for poised self-respect.

Love affirmations: attract, don't chase

  • "I am naturally magnetic."
  • "I know exactly what I bring to a relationship."
  • "I don't compete for attention, and I don't beg for affection."
  • "I am treated with kindness and respect."
  • "I receive affection with an open heart."

From Love Looks Good On Me (a whispered night CD in six parts, free) and Princess Treatment (free).

How to use love affirmations without getting delulu

  • Affirm qualities, not a specific person. "I attract kind, consistent love" beats "He texts me back by 9."
  • Let them shape your standards. Hearing "I stay where I am valued" every night makes it easier to actually leave where you're not.
  • Love yourself out loud. Take yourself on a date. Buy the flowers. Princess treatment starts at home.

Honest note

Affirmations can help you feel worthy, but they can't fix a relationship that hurts you. If you feel unsafe or stuck, reach out to a friend you trust or a professional. You deserve real support.

Quick questions

What is a good self-love affirmation?

'I am more than enough, even on quiet days' is a beautiful one, because it doesn't depend on achieving anything.

Can affirmations help you attract love?

They can help you feel worthy, raise your standards and show up more open, which changes who you attract and who you keep. They can't control another person.

Should I say love affirmations about a specific person?

It's healthier to affirm the qualities you want in love, like kindness and consistency, than to focus on one specific person.
