Confidence Affirmations for Main Character Energy

GEGül Edayour BFF & the girl who made DIVA
Published November 14, 20263 min read
✦ The quick answer, babe

Confidence affirmations work best when they describe ease, not perfection: 'I take up space with ease', 'My energy is one of a kind', 'I don't compete. I set the mood.' Listen to them daily and right before big moments, and pair them with posture: shoulders back, slow breath, chin soft.

Main character energy isn't being the loudest girl in the room. It's being the girl who's completely at home in herself. Unbothered. Magnetic. Mildly iconic.

Confidence affirmations I swear by

  • "My energy is one of a kind."
  • "I don't compete. I set the mood."
  • "My presence is the entire entrance."
  • "I take up space with ease."
  • "I was never ordinary. That's the point."
  • "I am magnetic because I am myself."

All from That Girl Energy, the first CD I ever made for DIVA. It's free. Obviously.

When to use confidence affirmations

  • Getting ready. Put it on while you do your makeup. Glow with the audio.
  • Before something big. Interview, date, presentation, the gym you're scared of. Five minutes in the car.
  • After a wobble. Somebody said something weird? Press play and come back to yourself.

Pair it with your body

Confidence lives in posture as much as in words. As each line plays: shoulders back, chin soft, slow exhale. That's literally how DIVA's That Girl Energy starts: "Hey, diva. Shoulders back, chin soft. This one is all you."

"Fake it till you make it"?

I'd rather say "try it on till it fits." Confidence affirmations aren't about pretending to be someone else; they're about remembering who you are when you're not doubting yourself. If a line feels like a costume, swap it for one that feels like your favourite jeans.

Quick questions

What are good confidence affirmations?

Short, present-tense lines about ease and self-trust, such as 'I take up space with ease', 'My energy is one of a kind' and 'I trust my ideas'.

How do I build main character energy?

Start your day on purpose, dress for yourself, speak kindly to yourself and stop competing. Daily confidence affirmations help you rehearse that feeling.

How often should I listen to confidence affirmations?

Daily for a few weeks, plus right before big moments. Consistency matters more than length.
