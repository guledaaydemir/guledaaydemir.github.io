Quick fixes

I don't hear anything when I press play

Turn the volume up with the side buttons while a CD is playing, check that your headphones or Bluetooth speaker are connected (or disconnected), and make sure no other app is holding the audio. Still silent? Force-quit DIVA and reopen it. If it keeps happening, email me with the CD name and voice.

Playback stops when I lock my phone

DIVA keeps playing in the background with lock screen controls. If it stops early, check whether a sleep timer is set (you'll find it in the player), or whether another app, a call or an alarm took over the audio. Press play on the lock screen to continue.

My morning or bedtime reminder doesn't show up

Open Settings → Notifications → DIVA on your iPhone and allow notifications. Then in DIVA's Settings, check the reminder is on and the time is right. Focus modes can also silence reminders.

How do I change the voice or soundscape?

In the player, tap the voice or soundscape button and pick another one. Your choice is remembered for next time. Runway, After Dark and most soundscapes need DIVA PRO.

How do I change the language?

DIVA starts in your iPhone's language (English or Turkish). To switch, open Settings → Language inside DIVA. CDs are narrated in English for now, whatever the app language.

I bought DIVA PRO but it's not unlocked

Go to Settings → See DIVA PRO → Restore Purchases in DIVA, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Check your internet connection the first time.

How do I cancel DIVA PRO?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DIVA PRO → Cancel Subscription. You keep PRO until the end of the paid period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

All App Store payments are handled by Apple, so request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. I don't have access to your payment details.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives on your iPhone. In DIVA open Settings → Storage & data to clear listening history, delete your creations or delete your Manifest List (your Wishlist, its photos and notes). Deleting the app removes all of it.

Will my Wishlist move to my new iPhone?

DIVA has no account or cloud of its own, so your data moves only if you transfer it with an iPhone backup or Quick Start when you set up the new phone.
