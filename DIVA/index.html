A note from your BFF

Why I made DIVA

Okay, story time. I was lying on my floor at 1am, scrolling affirmation videos that sounded like a GPS reading a fortune cookie. Robot voice. Beige background. Zero sparkle. And I thought: excuse me, where is the glamour?

I grew up on 2000s supermodel energy. Flip phones, lip gloss, leopard everything, girls who walked into a room like the room should be grateful. I wanted affirmations that felt like that. Like a CD you'd put on while getting ready for the night of your life.

So I built it. Every theme is a CD. The voices are warm and soft. The lines are written to feel true in your mouth, not cringe. And it's private, because your dreams are nobody's business but yours.

I won't pretend an app can hand you a penthouse. But I know what it feels like to start the day hearing "my presence is the entire entrance" instead of my own doom-scroll. That's the whole point. Press play. Pick yourself.

xoxo, Gül Eda

FAQ

Questions? I've got answers.

What is DIVA?

DIVA is an affirmation and manifestation app for iPhone made by independent developer Gül Eda Aydemir. Every theme is an affirmation CD, like That Girl Energy, Rich Girl Frequency or Beauty Sleep for Divas. You pick a CD, choose a voice and a soundscape, and listen to calm, first-person affirmations for 10, 20 or 60 minutes. It also has Morning and Bedtime affirmation meditations, a Make Your Own CD feature and a Wishlist with a vision board.

Is DIVA free?

Yes. DIVA is free to download and the free version includes 12 CDs with 10 minute sessions, the Morning and Bedtime rituals (5 and 10 minutes), two voices, two soundscapes, up to 3 custom CDs and up to 5 active dreams on your Wishlist. DIVA PRO is optional and unlocks everything else. No ads, ever, in either version.

Who made DIVA?

Me! I'm Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent iOS developer. I design, write and build DIVA myself. No big company, no investors, just a girl with a leopard-print laptop sleeve and opinions about how affirmations should sound.

Which devices does DIVA work on?

DIVA is an iPhone app and needs iOS 17 or later. There's no Android or web version right now.

What languages is DIVA in?

The app's menus and text are in English and Turkish, and the CDs are narrated in English for now (Turkish narration is paused). DIVA follows your iPhone's language, and you can switch in Settings. This website is also in Spanish, but the app itself doesn't speak Spanish yet.

Do I need an account?

Nope. No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and press play.
