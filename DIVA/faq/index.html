About DIVA

What is DIVA?

DIVA is an affirmation and manifestation app for iPhone made by independent developer Gül Eda Aydemir. Every theme is an affirmation CD, like That Girl Energy, Rich Girl Frequency or Beauty Sleep for Divas. You pick a CD, choose a voice and a soundscape, and listen to calm, first-person affirmations for 10, 20 or 60 minutes. It also has Morning and Bedtime affirmation meditations, a Make Your Own CD feature and a Wishlist with a vision board.

Is DIVA free?

Yes. DIVA is free to download and the free version includes 12 CDs with 10 minute sessions, the Morning and Bedtime rituals (5 and 10 minutes), two voices, two soundscapes, up to 3 custom CDs and up to 5 active dreams on your Wishlist. DIVA PRO is optional and unlocks everything else. No ads, ever, in either version.

Who made DIVA?

Me! I'm Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent iOS developer. I design, write and build DIVA myself. No big company, no investors, just a girl with a leopard-print laptop sleeve and opinions about how affirmations should sound.

Which devices does DIVA work on?

DIVA is an iPhone app and needs iOS 17 or later. There's no Android or web version right now.

What languages is DIVA in?

The app's menus and text are in English and Turkish, and the CDs are narrated in English for now (Turkish narration is paused). DIVA follows your iPhone's language, and you can switch in Settings. This website is also in Spanish, but the app itself doesn't speak Spanish yet.

Do I need an account?

Nope. No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and press play.

Listening

How long are DIVA sessions?

CDs play as 10, 20 or 60 minute sessions. Free CDs give you 10 minutes; 20 and 60 minutes come with DIVA PRO. The Morning and Bedtime rituals come in 5, 10 and 20 minutes (5 and 10 are free). PRO CDs have a short preview so you can hear them first.

Can I listen with my screen locked or while I fall asleep?

Yes. DIVA keeps playing in the background and you get lock screen controls. There's a sleep timer, and the Bedtime ritual opens in a dim night player. Keep the volume comfortable, especially with headphones at night.

Does DIVA work offline?

Yes. All CDs and voices are built into the app, so you can listen on a plane, in the subway or in a cabin with zero bars.

Are the voices real people?

No. DIVA's narration is AI-generated speech. The voices (Velvet Whisper, Pink Muse, Runway and After Dark) are not recordings of real people and don't imitate anyone. Affirmations you write in your own words are voiced on your iPhone.

Can I make my own affirmation CD?

Yes! In the Create tab you pick the affirmations that sound like you and save them to My CDs. Free users can keep up to 3 custom CDs. Writing affirmations in your own words is a DIVA PRO feature.

What are the soundscapes, and do the 528 Hz tones do anything?

Soundscapes are the music bed under the voice: Pink Aura, Ocean at 2AM, Hotel Rain, Dreamy Static, Silk Noise, Celestial Drift and soft tones at 111, 432, 528, 639, 777, 888 and 963 Hz. Honest answer about the tones: their names describe a mood, not an effect. They're there because they sound dreamy, not because they heal anything.

What are the Morning and Bedtime rituals?

Two guided affirmation meditations at the top of My CDs. You breathe, settle in and hear the day's affirmations with space to repeat them in your head. Each day of the week has its own theme, like Confidence on Tuesday or Gratitude on Sunday. Optional reminders open straight into the ritual.

Wishlist & vision board

What is the Wishlist in DIVA?

It's your manifestation list. Write down the dreams you're calling in (travel, home, career, love, money), track progress, celebrate milestones and mark them achieved. You can add your own photos to build a vision board, link each dream to a matching CD and turn on a daily vision reminder. Free users can keep up to 5 dreams on their way at once; PRO is unlimited.

Is my vision board private?

Completely. Your dreams, notes and photos are stored only on your iPhone. DIVA uses the camera only when you choose to snap a picture for your board, and saves an achievement card to your photos only when you tap save.

DIVA PRO & billing

What does DIVA PRO include?

Every CD in the collection, full 20 and 60 minute sessions, all four voices and every soundscape, writing affirmations in your own words, the Chrome Angel and Leopard Luxe themes, unlimited dreams and every vision board layout.

How much is DIVA PRO?

At launch in the US: $5.99 a month, $29.99 a year, or $69.99 once for Lifetime. The App Store shows the exact price in your country and currency before you confirm.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DIVA PRO → Cancel Subscription. You keep PRO until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open DIVA, go to Settings → See DIVA PRO → Restore Purchases. Lifetime and active subscriptions come right back.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple, so refunds do too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. I can't see or refund App Store payments myself, but email me if something went wrong and I'll help however I can.

Privacy & honesty

Does DIVA collect my data?

No. DIVA has no account, no ads, no analytics SDK and no server. Favorites, history, your CDs, your dreams and your photos stay on your iPhone. Purchases are handled by Apple. Read the full privacy policy.

Do affirmations actually work?

Real talk: affirmations are a tool for focus and mood, not a magic spell. Lots of people find that repeating kind, specific, present-tense sentences helps them start the day calmer or notice their own wins. Research on self-affirmation is promising in some areas and mixed in others. DIVA doesn't promise outcomes; it gives you a ritual that feels good enough to actually keep.

Is DIVA therapy or medical advice?

No. DIVA is for personal reflection and relaxation. It isn't medical, psychological or financial advice, and the Bedtime ritual is a wind-down, not a sleep treatment. If you're going through something heavy, please talk to a professional. You deserve real support, babe.
