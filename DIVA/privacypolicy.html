Privacy Policy

The short version: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your affirmations, your Wishlist and your photos stay on your iPhone. DIVA only sends anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), so I can see which CDs you love and what to improve.

Updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the DIVA app for iPhone ("DIVA", "the app") handles information. DIVA is made by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent developer ("I", "me").

1. Information DIVA collects

DIVA has no account system, no advertising and no server of its own. It never collects the affirmations or CDs you write, your Wishlist, notes or photos.

To understand how DIVA is used and make it better, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google:

  • which screens and CDs are opened and played, how far a session is listened to, and choices such as voice, soundscape, theme and language;
  • steps around DIVA PRO: when the offer is shown, which plan is chosen, and whether a purchase, free trial, renewal, cancellation or refund happened (never your name or payment details);
  • basic technical information Firebase collects automatically: device model, iOS and app version, language, a random app-installation identifier and an approximate region derived from the IP address.

These statistics are not linked to your name, email or Apple Account. DIVA does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), turns ad personalization off, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google processes this data on my behalf under its Firebase privacy terms and keeps it only for a limited time. DIVA does not sell or share personal data. If you want the statistics from your installation deleted, email me (see section 10).

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, DIVA saves some things locally on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage:

  • your favorites, listening history and preferences (voice, soundscape, theme, language, reminder times);
  • CDs you create and affirmations you write;
  • your Wishlist (Manifest List): dreams, notes, progress, milestones and any photos you add to your vision board;
  • prepared narration audio, cached so CDs start instantly.

This information never leaves your device through DIVA. It may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which are controlled by you and Apple. Affirmations you write in your own words are turned into speech on your iPhone.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for your vision board.
  • Photos: you pick photos for your vision board with the iOS photo picker, which shares only the photos you select. DIVA asks to add to your photo library only when you choose to save an achievement card.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. They are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.

You can change these permissions any time in iPhone Settings.

4. Purchases

DIVA PRO subscriptions and the Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your name, email or payment details. Apple gives developers anonymous, aggregated sales reports, and DIVA records the purchase steps described in section 1 without any personal details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your Apple Account.

5. Information Apple may share

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iPhone Settings, Apple may provide me with anonymous crash reports and aggregate usage statistics. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

6. Deleting your data

In DIVA open Settings → Storage & data to clear your listening history, delete your creations or delete your Manifest List. Deleting the app removes everything DIVA stored on your iPhone.

7. Children

DIVA does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous usage statistics described in section 1 are the same for every user and are not used to identify anyone.

8. Wellness disclaimer

DIVA is for personal reflection and relaxation. It is not medical, psychological or financial advice and does not promise outcomes. The narration is AI-generated speech and does not imitate any real person.

9. Changes to this policy

If DIVA ever changes how it handles information (for example, adding optional cloud sync), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

10. Contact

Questions? Email solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.

11. This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The demo audio plays straight from this site, and nothing you do on these pages is sent anywhere.

DIVA is free to download on iPhone. Pick a CD, press play and hear your dream life.
