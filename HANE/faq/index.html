About the game

What is HANE?

HANE is a tea-house management game (an arcade-idle tycoon) for iPhone and iPad. You play the çırak, the apprentice of a traditional Turkish kıraathane in İstanbul: take orders, call them out to the usta, carry tea on a hanging tray, collect bills, wipe tables, see the neighbourhood cats out and grow one small room into tea houses across Türkiye.

What is a kıraathane?

A kıraathane (literally a "reading house") is a traditional Turkish coffeehouse and tea house where neighbours meet over tea, conversation and table games such as okey, tavla and card games. Read the full guide.

Who makes HANE?

HANE is made by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent iOS developer.

Which devices does HANE run on?

HANE is made for iPhone and iPad and is available on the App Store.

What languages is HANE in?

The game itself is in Turkish and English, and you can switch under Settings (Ayarlar). This website is available in Turkish, English and Spanish.

Is there a story?

Yes: the çırak's dream. Every evening he puts his wage of 15 jeton and half of the day's tips into his kumbara, saving for a car that costs 10,000 jeton. A dream page shows him walking the road toward it. Read the story.

Playing

How do I move the çırak?

Drag anywhere on the scene to walk. Stand next to a table, the counter, the games shelf, a cat or a flower pot for a moment and the çırak does what is needed there.

I took an order but the usta isn't making the tea. Why?

Orders go onto the çırak's notepad first. Walk to the counter and call them out: the usta only brews what has been called out. Then lift the ready glasses onto your tray and carry each to the table that ordered it.

How long does a game day last?

About four minutes of play. Then you get a day report and the game waits on a small "Ready?" card until you start the next day, which makes it a natural place to stop.

Why can't my guests be served?

Ingredients run out. Tap the basket over the scene to open the Market, fill the basket and send the çırak. While he is away, guests wait unless you have hired waiters.

What do I do about the cats?

Walk up to a cat to see it out before it knocks something over, or buy cat food and put a bowl down. A cat that is fed often starts to feel at home: it is harder to see out and may leave a mess to sweep up.

What happens when a quarrel breaks out?

You choose: calm it yourself (it may fail), call the usta (always works, but takes a while) or call the zabıta, the municipal police, who walk in from the street.

Can I play okey or tavla in HANE?

No. In HANE you run the tea house: you hand out okey sets, card decks and tavla boards to the tables, and find an okey table its fourth player. The regulars play; you serve them.

How do I open a new tea house?

Open the map, tap a city's pin and open it once you have enough jeton and reputation stars. Kadıköy, the second branch, costs 300 jeton and one star, so most players reach it within the first few days.

How does the bank loan work?

If a branch costs more than you have, the bank lends up to 70% of the price. A tenth of the loan is due every day (send the usta to the bank). Interest of 2% is added each evening, 5% more when an instalment is missed, and after three missed days in a row the zabıta close the branch the loan paid for.

Can I hire help?

Yes, up to three waiters per tea house. They work on their own at about half the çırak's speed, and training makes them quicker. The cat and the quarrels stay your job.

Offline, purchases and privacy

Does HANE need the internet?

No. HANE plays fully offline. It never connects to the internet.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads and no in-app purchases. Every chair, tray, waiter and tea house is earned in the game.

Does HANE collect any data?

No. HANE has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. See the privacy policy.

Will I lose my progress?

The game saves itself on your device. Deleting the game deletes the save. Your iPhone or iPad backups (on your computer or in iCloud) include it.

Is HANE good for short breaks?

A game day lasts about four minutes and then waits for you, so one day fits neatly into a short break. Our guide on game breaks and productivity explains how to keep a break a break, and our checklist for break-friendly games shows what to look for.
