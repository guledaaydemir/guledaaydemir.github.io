Quick fixes

The usta isn't making the tea.

Orders must be called out at the counter first. Walk to the counter after taking orders; the usta only brews what was called out.

Guests are waiting and nothing is being made.

Something may have run out. Check the basket over the scene: it shows what is left and says when you need to go to the market.

My tea house was closed by the zabıta.

Branches close when satisfaction stays very low after a warning, or when three loan instalments are missed in a row. Pay the fine or an instalment to reopen.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings (Ayarlar) at the bottom right and choose Turkish or English.

How do I turn off the music or sound effects?

Settings has separate switches for the music and the sound effects. Your choice is kept in the save.

Can I see the tutorial again?

Yes: Settings ▸ How to play.
