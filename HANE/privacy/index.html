Privacy policy

The short version: HANE does not collect, store or share any personal data. It never connects to the internet. Your game is saved only on your own device.

Effective November 16, 2026

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • Plays offline

1. Who I am

HANE ("the game") is made by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy covers the HANE app for iPhone and iPad and this website. Questions: solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.

2. What the game collects

Nothing. HANE has no sign-in, no servers, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service and no third-party SDK that collects data. The game makes no network connections.

3. What stays on your device

So that you can carry on where you left off, HANE saves your game on your iPhone or iPad: your coins, days played, tea houses, upgrades, the çırak's savings and your settings (language, music and sound effects). This file stays in the game's own storage on your device. I cannot see it. iOS may include it in your device or iCloud backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. Deleting the game deletes the save.

4. Children

HANE collects no information from anyone, including children.

5. Sharing and selling

There is nothing to share: no data leaves your device through HANE, and I do not sell or share any information.

6. Apple

You download HANE from Apple's App Store. Apple processes your purchase and download data, and may share anonymised, aggregated statistics with developers (for example, how many people opened the App Store page from this website, through the campaign tag in our links). This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

7. This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts load. The site remembers two small choices in your own browser's storage (that you cut the ribbon, and that you closed the language suggestion); they never leave your browser. The web demo runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

8. Your rights

Because neither the game nor this website collects personal data, there is nothing I hold about you to access, correct or delete. If you email me, I keep the conversation only to help you, and I will delete it on request. You have the rights given by the laws that apply to you, such as the GDPR or the KVKK.

9. Changes

If a future version changes any of this (for example, by adding analytics or purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

10. Contact

Gül Eda Aydemir · solostaseraofficial@gmail.com
