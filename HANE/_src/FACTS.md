# HANE fact sheet (source of truth for site copy, guides and llms.txt)

Checked against the game's code and README on 2026-11-16. Do not state anything about HANE that is not here.

## The app

- **HANE** is a tea-house management game (arcade-idle tycoon) for **iPhone and iPad**, by **Gül Eda Aydemir**,
  an independent iOS developer. Contact: solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.
- App Store names: EN "HANE: Turkish Tea House Tycoon", TR "HANE: Kahvehane İşletme Oyunu",
  ES "HANE: Juego de Cafetería Turca". App Store id 6835573103.
- **Plays fully offline. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. Collects no data** (no analytics, no
  tracking, no network code). The save stays on the device.
- The game's own text is in **Turkish and English** (switch under Ayarlar / Settings). It is not in Spanish.
- **No notifications, no energy or wait timers, no daily log-in rewards.** Nothing happens while the app is
  closed: the game saves when you leave and carries on from the same moment when you come back. (Checked in
  the code on 2026-11-16: no notification package or code; the clock is used only to stamp the save.)
- Do not state a price, release date, ratings, download counts or awards.

## What you do

- You are the **çırak** (apprentice, red fez) in a traditional Istanbul **kıraathane** (tea house).
  The **usta** (master, moustache) brews at the counter; in Üsküdar he is **Hasan Usta**.
- Drag anywhere to walk. Stand next to something for a moment and the çırak does what is needed there.
- A guest's way: take the order at the table (it goes on the çırak's notepad) → walk to the counter and
  call the orders out (the usta makes only what was called out) → lift the ready glasses onto the hanging
  tray (askılı tepsi; holds 2 at first) → put each item down at the table that ordered it → collect the
  bill → wipe the table (soap bubbles, a sparkle) → next group.
- Menu and prices in jeton (coins): strong tea (koyu) 10, light tea (açık) 10, water 8, orange / lemon /
  rosehip / cherry oralet 16, Turkish coffee 25, cheese toast (kaşarlı tost) 30. Ingredients: tea, coffee,
  bread, kaşar, oralet.
- **Table games**: okey, 52'lik (cards) and tavla (backgammon) tables. The çırak hands out the set from the
  games shelf and finds an okey table its fourth player. (You serve the players; you do not play okey yourself.)
- **A game day lasts about four minutes** of play (240 seconds). Then a day report, and the game waits on a
  small "Ready?" card until you start the next day: a natural stopping point.
- Regulars in flat caps; some guests are in a hurry; now and then a grandchild comes along.
- With no free table, up to four groups queue at the door with a face over each (smile → frown → leave).

## Incidents

- **Cats**: Mırmır, Pamuk, Aslan, Tekir, Karagöz. Walk up to see one out, or buy cat food and put a bowl
  down. A cat fed often feels at home: harder to see out, climbs onto tables sooner, may leave a mess the
  çırak sweeps up.
- **Quarrels** over a game: calm it yourself (may fail), call the usta (always works, takes a while) or the
  **zabıta** (municipal police), who walks in from the street.
- **Rain**: hand out umbrellas at the outdoor tables. **Flowers** get thirsty once a day at a random time;
  water them or they wilt and dent everyone's mood.
- The usta is sometimes called away; then the çırak brews by hand.
- Decision windows pause the game and show what each choice costs or risks.

## Growing

- **Market**: tea, coffee, bread, kaşar, oralet (four colours), cat food. The basket holds 4 packs at first
  (8 for 180 jeton, 12 for 650). While the çırak shops, guests wait unless waiters serve.
- **Upgrades** (Geliştir): chairs, tables, tray for 4 glasses (220) and 6 (900), double teapot set
  (çift çaydanlık), menu items, table games.
- **Waiters**: hire up to three; they work by themselves at about half the çırak's speed; trainings make
  them quicker and let them carry more. The cat and quarrels stay yours.
- **Satisfaction** 0-100 per branch. Prices can go up +10% at most once a day, up to +40%; the first step
  needs 60% satisfaction, each further one 10 points more; each step costs every guest 4 points.
  Below 40% at day's end the zabıta warn; below 25% after a warning they close the branch until a fine is paid.
- **Eight branches** on a map of Türkiye, each with its own usta and floor plan (some L-shaped; Bursa and
  Eskişehir have two storeys): Üsküdar (start, Hasan Usta), Kadıköy 300 jeton + 1 star (Rıza Usta),
  Beşiktaş 2,500 + 3 (Cemal Usta), Bursa 9,000 + 6 (İsmail Usta), İzmir 18,000 + 9 (Nuri Usta),
  Ankara 30,000 + 12 (Kemal Usta), Eskişehir 45,000 + 15 (Salih Usta), Trabzon 65,000 + 18 (Temel Usta).
- **Every branch opens with a ribbon**: swipe through it like scissors, confetti, "Dükkânı aç".
- **The bank** lends up to 70% of a branch's price. A tenth of the loan is due each day (send the usta to
  the bank); 2% interest each evening, 5% more when the day's instalment was not paid; three unpaid days in
  a row and the zabıta close the branch the loan paid for.
- The first day is quick and generous: small coin rewards for each first (shopping, order, tea, bill,
  wiping, watering, cat). The second branch is reachable within the first few days.
- From a branch's sixth day guests come more often each day (more running, more earnings).
- Turn the venue round with the arrow buttons; walls step aside.

## The çırak's dream (Çırağın Hayali)

- The çırak dreams of **a car of his own, costing 10,000 jeton**.
- Every evening his **wage from the usta (15 jeton) and half the day's tips** go into his **kumbara**
  (piggy bank).
- The dream page opens after the third day: a painting of him dreaming of the car, a road from his kumbara
  to the car with him walking along it as far as his savings reach (the start of the road is stretched so the
  first evenings visibly move him), **flags at 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 and 75 percent**, saved and still to save,
  and what went in last evening and over the last seven days. Passing a flag is announced in the evening.
- Later opened from the car button (Hayal) over the scene or the day report's kumbara line.

## The look and sound

Hand-illustrated characters; dark wood, brass edges, red ribbon titles, parchment; a kilim on the floor;
an old Istanbul street with plane trees, cypresses, old street lamps, a simit cart, the neighbourhood
fountain, parked cars, taxis and dolmuş minibuses driving round; kıraathane music (switchable) and sound
effects (switchable).
