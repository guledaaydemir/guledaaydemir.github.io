SHOOTS

Be the remote

Your friend's phone is the camera. This one starts the shots, from wherever you're standing. Being the remote is free.

  1. 01

    Get SHOOTS

    Already have it? Skip to step 2.

    Download on the App Store
  2. 02

    Open it as the remote

    No camera access or sign-up needed. Or open SHOOTS and tap “Use as a remote” on the first screen.

    Open SHOOTS as a remote
  3. 03

    Tap Allow on the camera

    Pick your friend's camera from the list. They tap Allow once, and you're connected. Keep both devices on Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
