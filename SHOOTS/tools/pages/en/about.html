About

No photographer?
No problem.

SHOOTS exists for everyone who has handed their phone to a stranger, got back one blurry frame with a thumb in it, and smiled anyway. It's a camera that keeps shooting while you pose, so you can be your own photographer.

The idea

Most photos of us are taken by someone else, in a rush, in one try. Self-timers help, but they were built for one moment: a countdown, a click, and a sprint back to the phone. A real photoshoot is the opposite: dozens of frames, small changes between each, and someone keeping the energy up.

SHOOTS gives you that from a propped-up iPhone. Pick a delay, a rhythm and how many photos you want, step into frame and pose. It counts you in, fires shot after shot, tells you when it's a wrap and, if you want, cheers you on while you work your angles. An Apple Watch turns into the remote.

The look

The flash-yellow, the black, the paparazzi flashes and the orange date stamp come from the 2000s: point-and-shoot digicams, red-carpet arrivals, photos that were loud on purpose. Open the app and the logo arrives like a celebrity: the autofocus hunts, the flashes go off and the money shot prints it on yellow paper.

The principles

  • Your photos stay yours. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. SHOOTS makes no network requests of its own.
  • Hands-free means hands-free. Once you tap the shutter, you shouldn't need to touch the phone again until the shoot is done.
  • Honest about what's free. The core shoot is free. SHOOTS+ is clearly marked everywhere it applies.
  • Kind to your camera roll. Every shoot gets its own album, and the sister apps help you post the best and clear the rest.

Who makes it

Made by Gül Eda

SHOOTS is designed, built and supported by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent iOS developer. She makes small apps that each fix one everyday problem, and answers support email herself.

Her other apps include DUMP (photo dumps and carousels), Solo.Swipe (camera roll cleanup), Solo.Shots (progress photos and timelapses), Solora (a role-based planner) and ATHA (breathing exercises). See all apps.
