Privacy policy

The short version: SHOOTS doesn't collect any data about you. Your photos are taken and stored on your own devices, and they only leave them when you choose to share them.

  • No account
  • No ads
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • Photos stay on device

Effective 22 October 2026 · updated 24 October 2026 · applies to SHOOTS for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, version 1.0 and later

This policy explains how the SHOOTS app ("SHOOTS", "the app") handles information. SHOOTS is made by Gül Eda Aydemir, an independent developer ("I", "me"). If anything here is unclear, email solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.

1. What I collect

Nothing. SHOOTS has no account or sign-in, no servers of its own, and it does not contain any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software from third parties. The app makes no internet requests of its own; the only connections it makes are directly between your own devices, when you use a remote. I don't receive your photos, videos, settings, device identifiers, location or usage information, and I can't see how you use the app.

2. What stays on your device

InformationWhere it livesWho can see it

Shoot sessions, favorites and TrashA database inside the app's private storageOnly you

Photos kept "App Only" are stored in a folder that SHOOTS excludes from iCloud and device backups, so they stay on that one device. Deleting the app deletes them. Items you delete in SHOOTS move to its Trash and are removed for good after 30 days, or sooner if you empty them yourself. Photos you save to your Photos library follow your Photos and iCloud settings, which are managed by Apple.

3. Permissions and why they're asked

  • Camera — to take your photos and videos. Required for the app to work.
  • Microphone — only when you use a mode that records sound (Video, the + Video modes, Cinematic, Live), or to listen for “Cheese” if you turn on the voice start. Not needed for photos.
  • Speech recognition — only for the “Cheese” voice start. Recognition runs on your device; what you say isn't recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local network — only to use another iPhone or iPad as a remote. Your devices find each other nearby and connect directly.
  • Photos — only to save your shots to your library and put each shoot in its own album, when you choose that option. SHOOTS does not upload or analyze your library.

You can change these at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. SHOOTS does not ask for your location, contacts or notifications, and it does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it doesn't track you.

4. On-device image and voice analysis

To start a shoot when you raise your palm, and for Pose Coach to know whether you're in close-up, full body or out of frame, SHOOTS looks at the camera picture using Apple's on-device Vision framework while the camera is open. For the “Cheese” start it uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. This happens only on your device, nothing from it is saved, and it isn't used for anything else.

5. Apple Watch and remotes

The SHOOTS Watch app communicates directly with SHOOTS on your own iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. If you use another iPhone or iPad as a remote, the two devices connect directly to each other over your local network (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth nearby), and a device can only connect after you tap Allow on the camera. Commands (start, stop, zoom, lens and mode) and, with SHOOTS+, the live viewfinder preview frames travel between your devices only. They never reach me or any server.

6. Purchases

SHOOTS+ is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether your subscription is active. I don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

7. Links to other apps and websites

SHOOTS can open the App Store pages of my other apps (DUMP, Solo.Swipe), Apple's subscription settings, and this website. Those pages are run by Apple or are covered by their own policies. Other apps of mine have their own privacy policies, linked on their websites.

8. Sharing

When you share a photo from SHOOTS, iOS shows its standard share sheet and you choose where it goes. What happens next is governed by the app or service you pick.

9. Children

SHOOTS is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Because I don't collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct, export or delete. Everything SHOOTS stores is on your device, under your control: delete photos in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA still give you rights; if you have a question about them, email me.

11. Changes

If SHOOTS ever changes how it handles information (for example, if it added a feature that needs a server), this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date at the top, and the App Store privacy details will change to match.
