The soil check

A soil check, never an order

Most houseplants are lost to too much water, not too little. So when a plant's interval is up, MULA doesn't say “water now”. It asks how the soil feels, and your answer decides.

  • Only “Dry” records a watering, so the history shows what really happened.
  • Answer from the notification, a widget, your Apple Watch or Siri, without opening the app.
  • A check that waits too long becomes Emergency Care after 7 days. You choose the number.

    The name

    MULA means root

    MULA comes from Mūlādhāra, the root chakra in Sanskrit: mūla means root, ādhāra means support. It is the first of the seven, the one that keeps us grounded, safe and steady.

    A plant shows its leaves, but it lives through its roots. That is why MULA never orders a watering. It asks you to touch the soil first, and lets the answer decide.

    Questions, answered

    What is MULA?

    MULA is a calm plant and flower care app for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch app, widgets and Siri. It reminds you to check the soil instead of telling you to water on a fixed day, follows the seasons for each plant and keeps a photo journal. No ads, no account.

    Is MULA free?

    Yes. MULA is free to download and the free version keeps up to 10 plants with all reminders, soil checks and seasonal plans. MULA Premium (Annual with a 1-week free trial, or Lifetime) adds unlimited plants, plan editing, Vacation mode, the light meter, frost and heat alerts and more.

    How does MULA know when to water?

    It doesn’t guess. Each plant has a soil-check interval for the current season. When it is up, MULA asks how the soil feels: Dry logs a watering, A little moist asks again in 2 days, Wet skips the round.

    Can MULA identify my plant?

    It can suggest the kind of plant from a photo, on your iPhone: orchid, rose, tulip, cactus, fern and about 20 kinds in total. It does not identify exact species or diagnose diseases, and it says so when it isn’t sure.

    Does MULA need an account or the internet?

    No account. MULA works offline. When you are online it backs up and syncs your garden through your own iCloud, sends anonymous usage statistics, and, if you turn them on, asks Apple Weather for frost and heat alerts.

    Which devices does MULA work on?

    iPhone with iOS 17 or later, iPad with iPadOS 17 or later, Macs with Apple silicon (as the iPad app), and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. With Sync with iCloud, they all show the same garden.

    Is the app in Turkish or Spanish?

    The app’s interface is in English for now. This website is in English, Turkish and Spanish.
