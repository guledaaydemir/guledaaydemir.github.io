Quick fixes

Reminders don’t arrive
  • Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → MULA is allowed.
  • In MULA, open Settings → Reminders & quiet hours and check the switches and the reminder time.
  • Reminders never come during quiet hours, and only once a day per plant.
  • Focus modes can hold notifications back; allow MULA in your Focus settings.
  • Open MULA once after changing the time zone so reminders are rebuilt.
Widget buttons don’t answer

The answer buttons on medium and large widgets are part of MULA Premium. A button only records an answer while that task is still due; if you already answered somewhere else, it does nothing. Remove and add the widget again if it looks stale.

Apple Watch shows nothing

Open MULA on your iPhone once so it can send the latest list to the watch. If the iPhone isn’t nearby, answers from the watch wait and are applied when it is. Answering from the watch is Premium.

Frost and heat alerts

They need MULA Premium, approximate location permission and an internet connection for Apple Weather. Alerts are only for plants set to Balcony or Outdoor. You can check the time of the last update in Settings → Reminders & quiet hours.

The light meter reads oddly

Hold the iPhone where the plant will live with the back camera facing the window, and give it a moment to settle. The reading is an estimate; the thresholds (about 800 and 10,000 lux) are rough guides, not exact science.

Restore Premium

Open Settings → MULA Premium → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

New iPhone

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and install MULA. If your garden was backed up (Settings → iCloud backup), MULA offers to restore it before the intro. You can also restore it later from Settings → iCloud backup. Premium comes back with Settings → MULA Premium → Restore purchases.

Delete everything

Deleting the MULA app removes your plants, photos and history from the iPhone. Your iCloud backup and synced garden stay in your own iCloud, so they can come back; to remove them, delete MULA’s data in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → iCloud storage. Nothing is stored with me to delete.
