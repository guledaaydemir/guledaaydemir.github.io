Questions about MULA

Using MULA

How do I add a plant?

Tap + in Garden or on Today. Give it a name, choose a species (or keep the general care plan), say where it lives (Indoor, Balcony or Outdoor) and how much light it gets. MULA suggests a gentle starting plan you can change later.

Why does MULA ask about the soil instead of telling me to water?

Because the soil knows better than a calendar. A watering is only logged when you say the soil was dry; “A little moist” asks again in 2 days and “Wet” skips the round. This avoids the most common houseplant problem: too much water.

What is Emergency Care?

When a soil check has waited longer than your threshold (7 days by default, change it in Settings → Emergency care after), the plant moves to the top of Today with one extra nudge.

Which plants does MULA know?

20 plants with seasonal plans: monstera, camellia, rubber plant, hydrangea, moth orchid, Chinese money plant, snake plant, peace lily, pothos, lavender, aloe, rose, tulip, cactus, Boston fern, English ivy, begonia, poinsettia, rosemary and areca palm. Any other plant can use a general care plan you adjust.

Can MULA identify a plant from a photo?

It suggests the plant kind (about 20 kinds, such as orchid, rose, tulip, cactus, fern or palm) using Apple’s image recognition on your iPhone. It doesn’t name exact species and never diagnoses pests or diseases.

Does MULA follow the seasons in the Southern hemisphere?

Yes. Turn on Settings → Southern hemisphere and the seasons are reversed.

What happens while I’m on vacation?

With Premium, Vacation mode lets you pause reminders, keep them, or share a care sheet with someone who is helping. Nothing is marked as done for you; when you come back, MULA helps you see how everyone is.

Reminders, widgets & devices

When do reminders arrive?

At the time you choose in Settings → Reminders & quiet hours (9:00 by default), never during quiet hours (22:00–08:00 by default) and never more than once a day for the same plant.

Can I answer without opening the app?

Yes. Soil-check reminders have “Dry · I watered it”, “A little moist” and “Wet” buttons; feeding reminders have “Fed today” and “Later”. With Premium you can also answer from medium and large widgets and Apple Watch.

Which widgets are there?

Small (the next plant), Lock Screen (circular, rectangular and inline) — both free — plus medium and large widgets whose answer buttons are Premium.

Does MULA work with Siri?

Yes, with no setup: “I watered Momo in MULA”, “What needs care in MULA”, “Soil check for Momo in MULA”, “I fed Momo in MULA”. Phrases are in English.

Is there an iPad, Mac or Android version?

iPad and Mac, yes; Android, not at the moment. The same app runs on iPhone (iOS 17 or later), iPad (iPadOS 17 or later) and Macs with Apple silicon, where it shows a calm, phone-width column. Turn on Sync with iCloud and every device shows the same garden; Premium comes along with Restore purchases. The light meter needs a back camera, so it isn’t available on a Mac.

Is the app available in other languages?

The app’s interface is in English for now. This website is available in English, Turkish and Spanish.

Free & Premium

What is free?

Up to 10 plants with 5 photos each, all soil-check and feeding reminders, viewing seasonal plans, the seasonal temperature check, photo identification, small and Lock Screen widgets, the Apple Watch list, Siri, both themes and the sounds.

What does Premium add?

Unlimited plants and portraits, editing seasonal plans, the full photo journal and posters, Vacation mode and care sheets, the light meter, frost and heat alerts, and answer buttons on medium/large widgets and Apple Watch.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is Annual (with a 1-week free trial) or Lifetime (a single purchase). Prices depend on your country and are shown in the App Store before you buy.

How do I cancel or restore?

Cancel the Annual plan any time in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews. To restore a purchase, open Settings → MULA Premium → Restore purchases.

Are there ads?

No. Not in the free version, not in Premium, never.

Privacy & data

Where is my garden stored?

On your iPhone (or iPad or Mac), shared with MULA’s own widgets and Apple Watch app, and backed up and synced through your own iCloud, which only you can see. There is no MULA account and no MULA server.

Does MULA collect analytics?

Only anonymous usage statistics, through Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens are opened, which care is recorded and how many plants a garden has. Never plant names, notes, captions or photos, and no advertising ID or tracking. Turn it off in Settings → Your garden → “Share anonymous usage”.

Why does MULA ask for my location?

Only if you turn on frost and heat alerts (Premium). Your approximate location is rounded to about 1 km, kept on your iPhone, and only that rounded point is sent to Apple Weather for the forecast.

Are light-meter photos saved?

No. The light meter reads the camera’s exposure to estimate lux; no photo is taken or stored.

How do I move my garden to a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and install MULA. If your garden was backed up to iCloud (Settings → iCloud backup, on by default), MULA offers to bring it back before the intro, with its care history, photos and settings. Premium comes back with Restore purchases.

Can I use MULA on more than one device, or share my garden?

Yes. With “Sync with iCloud” on (Settings → Sync & sharing), every device signed in to your Apple Account shows the same garden. With Premium you can also invite a partner or flatmate to share it, so when one of you waters, the other’s reminder quietly goes away.
