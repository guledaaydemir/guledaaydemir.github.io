About the developer

Gül Eda Aydemir, independent iOS developer

Updated 15 October 2026

In short: Gül Eda Aydemir is an independent iOS developer who builds small iPhone and iPad apps that each solve one everyday problem. They are on the App Store under the seller name “Gul Eda Aydemir”: Solo.Swipe, Solora, DUMP, Solo.Shots, ATHA and the newest, SHOOTS (October 2026). She designs, builds and supports them herself, and you can email her at solostaseraofficial@gmail.com.

Who I am

Hi, I'm Gül Eda. I design, build and support these apps myself: I write the code in Swift, test it and answer the support email.

The first, Solo.Swipe, came out in April 2026. Since then I've shipped five more, each aimed at one specific everyday problem rather than trying to do everything. You can also find me on Instagram as @solo.stasera.

How I build them

  • One app, one problem. Each app is built around a short, repeatable session: a few minutes of swiping, one breath, three priorities, one photo.
  • No accounts. None of the apps asks you to sign up or log in.
  • On-device where it matters. Photo analysis in Solo.Swipe, DUMP and Solo.Shots runs on your iPhone, and Solora's AI assistant uses Apple's on-device models. Your photos are not uploaded to me.
  • Honest about money. ATHA is completely free. The others are free to download with optional paid tiers, and two of them (Solo.Swipe and Solo.Shots) show ads in the free version. The free-tier limits are in the home page facts table, and current prices are on each app's App Store page.

Support & contact

Email solostaseraofficial@gmail.com. It comes straight to me. To help me help you faster, include:

  • the app name and version (you'll find it in the app's Settings or About screen),
  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version,
  • what you expected to happen, and what happened instead (a screenshot helps).

For subscriptions and refunds: purchases are handled by Apple, so you can cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings under your name › Subscriptions, and request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Spotted a mistake on this site, or a claim that doesn't match the app? Please tell me and I'll fix it.
